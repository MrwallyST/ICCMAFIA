# Technical Report: The Daily & 4H Combo Trading Strategy

## 1. Strategy Overview and Core Philosophy
The "Daily & 4H Combo" is a professional swing trading methodology designed to eliminate market noise by anchoring all decisions in higher-timeframe structural shifts. As a mentor, I preach a "Simple Trading" philosophy: we do not guess; we wait for the higher timeframes to dictate the narrative.

The core objective is to identify the precise transition of market control. We observe areas where buyers previously stepped in (support/swing lows) and wait for sellers to demonstrate dominance by "bypassing" or breaking those established levels. By synthesizing the momentum of the Daily chart with the refined structural breaks of the 4-hour and 1-hour charts, we execute high-probability setups where the macro and micro trends converge.

## 2. The ICC Framework: Indication, Correction, Continuation
The "ICC" framework is our non-negotiable checklist for qualifying any entry. We seek this pattern on the Daily timeframe to set our bias, then look for nested ICC patterns on the 4H or 1H for execution.

### Indication: The Breakout
An Indication is a definitive breakout under a swing high or swing low. 
*   **Bearish Indication:** For the sell strategy described here, a bearish indication is specifically defined as the **breach of a Daily swing low**. This signifies that the market has refused to respect the buyers at that level, and sellers have gained enough strength to push price out of its previous range.

### Correction: The Creator vs. The Product
A Correction is the temporary counter-trend move that follows an Indication. To understand this, you must use the **Creator vs. Product** analogy:
*   **The Creator:** This is the specific high or low that initiated a price move. For example, a low that pushes price to a new high is the "Creator."
*   **The Product:** The resulting move or new high is the "Product."
*   **Structural Integrity:** During a correction, the "Product" must never exceed the "Creator." If price is in a bearish correction (moving up), it must not break the level that created the previous move down. If the Creator is broken, the structural thesis is dead.

### Continuation: Resuming the Trend
Continuation is the moment the correction phase expires and price aligns with the original Indication. For a sell, the price must stop printing bullish structures (higher highs/higher lows) and begin breaking lower-timeframe swing lows to resume the primary bearish trend.

## 3. Multi-Timeframe Alignment and Logic
We do not trade timeframes in isolation. We look for a **Confluence of Structures** where every timeframe "matches."

### Timeframe Roles in the Strategy
| Timeframe | Functional Purpose |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Daily** | **The Anchor:** Identifies major support, swing lows, and provides the primary Bearish Indication. |
| **4H / 1H** | **Refinement:** Used to identify the "Fight" between buyers and sellers and find nested ICC entries. |
| **15m** | **Micro-Structure:** Used to see the internal trend of the 1H/4H pullback (the sequence of lower lows/highs within the correction). |

### The Logic of Alignment
Alignment is achieved when the 15-minute micro-structure confirms the bearishness of the 1-hour and 4-hour charts. When the internal trend of the correction breaks, it signals that the larger trend is resuming. Furthermore, you must only trade "In Session." Volume is the fuel that breaks "Creators." Focus your execution within the **London and New York** sessions, as these provide the maximum volume necessary to liquidate historical levels.

## 4. Trade Execution and Risk Management
### Entry Criteria
You must only trigger a sell entry when the following conditions are met:
1.  **Daily Indication:** Price must have bypassed or broken a significant Daily swing low.
2.  **Structural Shift:** On the 1H or 4H, the price must transition from a corrective uptrend to a downtrend (breaking internal swing lows).
3.  **The Fight:** Identification of multiple lower highs, proving that sellers are fighting back at support levels.
4.  **Session Context:** Execution must align with London or New York volume.

### Stop Loss: Invalidation Logic
We use the "Creator" rule for stop loss placement. Your stop loss must be placed above the previous lower high—the "Creator" of the current move. This is logical: if the market breaks the level responsible for the current momentum, your structural thesis is invalidated, and you no longer want to be in the trade.

### Profit Targeting: Taking Out the Creator
Profit targets are not arbitrary. We target the **"Last Creation"** made by the market. The goal is to "take out" or liquidate the previous historical low—the original Creator that caused the last major move in the opposite direction. Price naturally gravitates toward these levels to clear out liquidity.

## 5. Case Study: The Day 105 Sell Entry
This trade perfectly illustrates the "Daily & 4H Combo" in action.

*   **Daily Context:** Price was sitting at a major Daily support level (swing low). While most retail traders were looking for buys, we waited for the "Indication"—the moment price bypassed this level.
*   **The 4H Fight:** On the 4-hour chart, price printed **six consecutive lower highs**. Even though price was at support, the "Fight" showed that buyers were exhausted. Sellers were aggressively pushing down, refusing to let price return to previous highs.
*   **1H Alignment:** The 1-hour timeframe broke its internal bullish structure, shifting from a correction back into a continuation.
*   **Execution:** The entry was taken during the **London session** (where volume was at its peak) after a 4H rejection. 
*   **Risk Management:** The stop loss was placed above the previous lower high (the Creator). 
*   **Outcome:** By targeting the "Last Creation" (the previous historical low), the trade achieved a clean **1:7 Risk-to-Reward ratio**.

## 6. Critical Rules for Strategy Success
*   **Don't Be Literal:** Do not take every movement too literally. Understand the *flow* of the trend (uptrend vs. downtrend) rather than just memorizing candle patterns. 
*   **Focus on the Present:** While we use historical levels for targets, you must prioritize what price is doing at the **current time**.
*   **The Creator Rule is Absolute:** In a sell scenario, no move should ever break higher than the original Creator. If it does, the trend has shifted.
*   **Patience for Structure:** You must wait for price to respect structure. If there is no clear ICC framework and no clear "Creator," there is no trade.
*   **Zero Indicators:** This strategy relies exclusively on price action and market structure. Indicators like RSI or MACD are lagging and will only distract you from the structural truth of the chart.
*   **Confluence is Key:** Everything must match. When the Daily, 4H, 1H, and 15m are all showing the same bearish intent, the trade becomes a high-probability event.
