# Day 105: Day Trading Crash Course #5 (Daily & 4H Combo)

### 1. Strategy Overview: The Daily & 4H Combo
To achieve professional-grade consistency, you must simplify your market analysis by prioritizing higher timeframe structures over lower timeframe noise. This strategy utilizes the "Daily and 4H Combo" to strip away complexity and reveal where the true momentum lies. **Trading off the daily timeframe is the non-negotiable foundation of this strategy; it provides the foundational bias and directional conviction for every entry taken on smaller timeframes.** As a mentor, I mandate that you look for "super bold" daily levels—areas where buyers or sellers have previously stepped in with massive momentum—before even considering a lower timeframe execution.

### 2. The ICC Framework: Indication, Correction, Continuation
To master this strategy, you must first master the three phases of the ICC framework. This is how we define the lifecycle of a trend:

*   **Indication**: This is not a mere price flicker. It is a decisive breakout under swing highs or swing lows, signaling that one side (buyers or sellers) has seized "complete control." In the 1-hour timeframe, we look for price to hold under these levels for at least two hours to confirm the shift in power.
*   **Correction**: A temporary counter-trend move. In a bearish trend, the correction is a bullish sequence of higher highs and higher lows. This phase represents the market "re-fueling" before the next leg.
*   **Continuation**: The resumption of the primary trend. This begins only when the correction fails to break the "original creator" of the move and price re-aligns with the initial indication.

### 3. Market Structure and the "Creator" Concept
To understand market structure, you must understand the relationship between the **Creator** and the **Product**. The "Creator" is the origin point—the specific swing high or low that caused a new move to happen. Every subsequent move is the "Product" of that Creator.

**The Golden Rule**: The Product must never invalidate the Creator. In a downtrend, no subsequent move (product) can be higher than the previous lower high (the creator). If price breaks above the Creator, the "creation" is invalidated, and a trend reversal is likely.

**Market Structure Signals**

| Condition | Market Signal |
| :--- | :--- |
| Price breaks breakout under a Swing Low | Bearish Indication (Sellers in Control) |
| Price Retesting the Creator | High-Probability Entry Zone |
| Price breaks above the Creator | Sign of Trend Reversal |
| Series of Lower Highs | Strong Bearish Sentiment |
| Price fails to make a new high during correction | End of Correction Phase |

### 4. Multi-Timeframe Alignment Process
Analyze the market from the top down. Everything must "mirror" the higher timeframe bias before you risk a single dollar.

1.  **Daily Timeframe**: Identify the "super bold" swing levels. Locate where the massive momentum originated (the old lows or highs).
2.  **4-Hour Timeframe**: Observe the ICC pattern. Use this to bridge the gap between the daily bias and the intraday execution, watching for the daily level to be bypassed.
3.  **1-Hour Timeframe**: Mandate this timeframe for detail. If the 4H is unclear, the 1H reveals the shift in control. Look for price to sit below/above a broken level for multiple hours to confirm the Indication.
4.  **15-Minute Timeframe**: This is your micro-trend mirror. You must literally see the sequence of lower lows and lower highs (in a sell) matching the 4H bias. When the 15m structure aligns with the 4H and Daily, you have a high-probability trade.

### 5. Execution and Entry Mechanics
Verify that the market is failing to create new highs during its correction. We enter when the market provides a "sign of bearishness," such as the break of a minor 15m swing low after a series of lower highs.

**Trade Setup Checklist**
- [ ] **Identify the Trend**: Ensure the Daily bias is clearly established.
- [ ] **Find the Indication**: Confirm a breakout that signifies a shift in control.
- [ ] **Wait for the Correction**: Observe the counter-trend move as it approaches the Creator.
- [ ] **Verify Correction Failure**: Identify the end of the correction via a rejection or a break of micro-structure.
- [ ] **Execute in Session**: While New York is the "volume king," high-probability entries often materialize during the London session or the transition into New York.

### 6. Risk Management and Stop Loss Placement
Protecting capital is about structural logic, not arbitrary numbers.

*   **Stop Loss Placement**: Apply the "Previous Lower High" rule. If you are entering on a suspected lower high, your stop loss **must** be placed above the previous lower high—the "Creator" of the current downward move. If price hits this level, your trade thesis is structurally invalidated.
*   **Targeting Methodology**: We target "old lows" or the "last creation made." We are looking for price to take out the very cause of the previous move. In high-probability setups, this logic allows for significant returns, such as a 1-to-7 risk-to-reward ratio.

### 7. Key Takeaways for Professional Psychology
*   **Focus on the Current Creation**: Do not get confused by distant historical data. Analyze what the current price action is doing relative to the most recent "Creators."
*   **Mandate Alignment**: Patience is your greatest asset. Wait until the Daily, 4H, 1H, and 15m timeframes are all telling the same story.
*   **Volume is Fuel**: Major structural breaks require volume. Limit your execution to the London and New York sessions when the "max volume" is available to push price through daily levels.
