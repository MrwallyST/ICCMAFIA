# Report: Day Trading Crash Course #5 – The Daily & 4H Combo Strategy

## 1. Strategy Overview: The Daily & 4H Combo
The "Daily & 4H Combo" is designed to strip away the noise of the markets, simplifying analysis by using higher time frames to identify momentum and structure. As a professional swing trader, my goal is to teach you how to align the broad market narrative with precise execution. 

Success in this game is not just about charts; it requires an **Entrepreneurial Mindset**. You must cultivate the "best psychology possible"—approaching every trade with discipline and patience. We don't chase the market; we wait for the market to reveal its intent through structure.

## 2. The Fundamentals of Market Structure

### The "Original Creator" and "Product"
To understand the market, you must understand the relationship between the **Creator** and the **Product**.
*   **The Creator:** The specific swing high or swing low that initiated a significant move.
*   **The Product:** The move itself (the expansion to a new high or low).

**The Golden Rule:** The "Product" should never break the level of its "Original Creator" unless a trend reversal is occurring.
*   **Bearish Logic:** The Creator is the high that produced a new low. Price should not trade higher than this point if the trend is to remain intact.
*   **Bullish Logic:** The Creator is the low that produced a new high. Price should not trade lower than this point if the trend is to remain intact.

### Identifying Swing Levels
*   **Swing Lows:** Points where selling momentum failed and buyers took control.
*   **Swing Highs:** Points where buying momentum failed and sellers took control.
*   **The Validation Rule:** For a sell entry to be valid, price **must** demonstrate the ability to bypass a swing low. This proves sellers have overcome the previous point of buyer strength.

## 3. The ICC Framework (Indication, Correction, Continuation)
Use this framework as your operational checklist. It is a universal language spoken by price across all timeframes.

| Phase | Description | Sign of Completion |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Indication** | A breakout move. Price moves under a swing low (bearish) or above a swing high (bullish). | A clear candle close beyond the structural level. |
| **Correction** | A counter-trend move. In a bearish setup, this is an uptrend consisting of higher highs and higher lows. | Price fails to make a new high and breaks a micro-swing low. |
| **Continuation** | Resumption of the original trend after the correction fails to break the "Original Creator." | Price resumes the direction of the initial Indication. |

## 4. Multi-Timeframe Analysis & Alignment
Consistency comes from alignment. If the timeframes are telling different stories, there is no trade.

*   **The Daily Time Frame:** This is your foundation. We use the Daily to identify major swing levels and set alerts. If price is at a Daily support, we are on high alert for a reaction.
*   **The 4-Hour Time Frame:** Our primary alignment timeframe. We look for the ICC process here to prepare for a break of the Daily structure.
*   **The 1-Hour Time Frame:** Scale down to the 1H when the 4H is "unclear" or lacks detail. For example, if the 1H shows sellers held control for two full hours under a level, that is a high-conviction signal that the 4H might not show as clearly.
*   **The 15-Minute Time Frame:** This is your micro-trend confirmation. Before we pull the trigger, the 15M must show a clear sequence of lower lows and lower highs that match our higher-timeframe bearish thesis.

> **Key Principle: Everything must align. A professional trade is only taken when the 15-minute, 1-hour, and 4-hour structures all point in the same direction as the Daily intent.**

## 5. Execution Tactics and Trade Management

### Entry Timing
Trade where the volume is. Execution should coincide with the **London or New York sessions**. Without session volume, the market lacks the momentum necessary to break major structural levels, leading to "fakeouts" and stagnation.

### Stop Loss Placement
Stop losses are based on structure, not pips. For a sell, your stop loss must be placed above the **previous lower high** (the "Creator" of the current move). If price hits this level, your thesis is invalidated, and you must exit.

### Trade Management and Targeting
*   **Targeting:** Aim for "old lows" or the levels that originally sparked the previous move. In our case study, this approach yielded a **1 to 7 Risk-to-Reward ratio**.
*   **Break-Even Logic:** Protect your capital. For instance, if price reaches a significant level like **4,692** (as seen in the current setup), the trade is secured at break-even. The trade remains valid as long as the structure holds.

## 6. Step-by-Step Case Study: The Bearish Setup
This is a "pro" sequence showing how to anticipate a move rather than just reacting to it:

1.  **Identify Daily Level:** Locate a major Daily swing low where buyers are sitting. 
2.  **Analyze the 4H/1H:** Observe that price has broken the 4H structure (Indication) and is currently in a Correction.
3.  **The 1H Confirmation:** Note that for at least two hours, sellers have held the price under a key level, showing they have regained control from the buyers.
4.  **Micro-Structure Alignment (The 15M Detail):** Leading into the **London session**, observe the 15-minute chart. Identify a series of **six consecutive lower highs**.
5.  **Anticipatory Execution:** Do not wait for the Daily level to break. Because the 15M micro-structure (the 6 lower highs) has already shifted, enter the short as price rejects the upside.
6.  **The Result:** The entry is taken *before* the Daily break, capitalizing on the momentum as the Daily level is eventually bypassed.

## 7. Conclusion: Strategic Takeaways
To succeed as a trader, you must adopt the mindset of an entrepreneur—disciplined, focused, and adaptable.
1.  **Focus on Current Action:** History gives context, but what price is doing *right now* is what pays you.
2.  **Structure Over Everything:** Indicators are secondary. Price will respect the "Creator" and the trend structure regardless of what an oscillator says.
3.  **Patience is Your Edge:** As a swing trader, your job is to set the trade based on alignment and then wait. If the structure hasn't broken your "Creator," the trade is still alive.
4.  **Don't Be Literal:** Trading is an art of identifying trends. Don't get bogged down in "perfect" candle closes; focus on identifying who is in control—the buyers or the sellers.
