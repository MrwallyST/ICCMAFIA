The Creator,"The original price movement source or pivot point (swing high/low) that caused a specific move; price should not break the ""original creator"" unless a reversal occurs.","The cause of a new high or low. For a buy, nothing should be lower than the original creator; for a sell, nothing should be higher.","Trace the current move back to its source. If price breaks the level of the original creator, it is a signal of a structural reversal.","Stop losses are placed above or below the previous creator level (e.g., above the previous lower high).","In a sell trade, identify the lower high that created the sell-off; place the stop loss above that ""creator"" point.",[1]
