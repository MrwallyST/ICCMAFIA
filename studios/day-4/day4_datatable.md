| Concept | Definition | Purpose in Trade |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **The Continuation** | Step 3: When price flips back into the original trend after a correction | To safely execute the trade in the main direction |
| **Market Cycle** | Indication ➡️ Correction ➡️ Continuation | The exact rhythm of a profitable market |
| **The X-Ray (1H Chart)** | The massive, overall directional setup | Used to find the main Indication |
| **The Scalpel (5m Chart)** | The precise internal structure | Used to pinpoint when the Correction is over |
| **Second Time Around** | Entering on the second push past a key level | Avoids fake-outs from the initial breakout |
| **Stop Loss** | Above/below the previous peak from the correction | Protects capital based on structure (Lower High/Higher Low) |
| **Take Profit** | The massive 1H level where Indication started | Aiming to secure profit on a brand new high/low |
