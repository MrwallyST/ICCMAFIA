## 🔬 The Surgeon Analogy: The Most Important Concept in Day 4

To nail a Continuation entry, you have to think like a surgeon. You don't perform surgery with just an X-Ray. You need two different instruments for two different purposes.

**The 1-Hour Chart is your X-Ray.** It tells you the overall direction of the market, where the Indication happened, and most importantly — the massive structural target that is your Take Profit. You use this chart to understand, not to trade.

**The 5-Minute Chart is your Scalpel.** After you know the big picture from the 1-Hour, you drop to the 5-Minute to watch the Correction form in detail. You watch the micro-structure — the Lower Highs, the Higher Lows — and you wait for the precise moment the Correction is ending.

That is when you enter.

---

## ⏳ The Second Time Around (The Most Important Rule)

Here is the rule that separates profitable traders from retail traders who constantly get stopped out:

**Never trade the first breakout of a level. Always wait for the second push.**

The first push past a level is a liquidity grab. It is the market sweeping your stop-losses if you went short, or triggering FOMO buyers who are now long at the worst possible moment. The market then pulls back, forming a Correction. Then — on the SECOND push past that key level on the 5-Minute chart — that is your entry.

This is the entry that the institution intended. You enter with precision, place your Stop Loss safely beyond the Correction's structural reference, and your Take Profit sits at the massive 1-Hour level.
