# 🧵 Twitter / Threads Breakdown: Continuations & The Entry Model

1/10 🚨 **THE MOMENT EVERY TRADE PAYS YOU**
The ICC Framework has 3 steps. Day 4 is the one where you actually get paid.

Step 1 (Indication) tells you the direction.
Step 2 (Correction) shakes out the amateurs.
Step 3 (Continuation) is where YOU enter and collect. Here's how. 👇

2/10 🔄 **THE CYCLE THAT NEVER STOPS**
Every profitable market runs the same cycle:

Indication → Correction → Continuation → Repeat.

The market breaks out, pulls back to trap retail, and then runs to its TRUE institutional destination. Learn to identify where you are in this cycle and you will always know what to do next.

3/10 🏥 **THE SURGEON ANALOGY**
To nail the perfect Continuation entry, you need to think like a surgeon.

A doctor doesn't just use an X-Ray to perform surgery. He uses precise instruments.

In trading:
1-Hour Chart = The X-Ray (macro direction & target)
5-Minute Chart = The Scalpel (precision entry)

4/10 🔭 **THE 1-HOUR X-RAY**
Your 1-Hour chart shows you:
✅ The overall trend direction (Uptrend or Downtrend)
✅ Where the Indication formed (the structural break)
✅ The massive 1H level that IS your Take Profit

You use this chart to understand the big picture — NOT to enter a trade.

5/10 🔬 **THE 5-MINUTE SCALPEL**
After confirming your setup on the 1H, you drop to the 5-Minute chart. This reveals:
✅ The micro-structure of the Correction (LH and HL patterns)
✅ The exact moment the Correction is stalling
✅ Your entry trigger — the second push past your key level

This is how you get precision entries without guessing.

6/10 ⏳ **THE SECOND TIME AROUND RULE**
This is the most important rule of Day 4:

🚫 NEVER trade the first breakout of a level.
✅ ALWAYS enter the SECOND time price pushes past your key level.

The first push is bait. It triggers retail stop-losses. The second push is the real institutional move. Be on the right side of it.

7/10 🛡️ **STOP LOSS PLACEMENT**
Once you enter on the Continuation:

Place your Stop Loss safely beyond the most recent structural peak formed during the Correction:
→ Long trade: Below the Higher Low of the Correction
→ Short trade: Above the Lower High of the Correction

If that level breaks, your setup was wrong. Respect the structure.

8/10 🎯 **TAKE PROFIT TARGET**
Your Take Profit is NOT a random number of points away from your entry.

It is the MASSIVE 1-Hour level where the Indication originally formed — the market's intended destination, where it will create a brand new High or Low.

This is the institutional target. Don't exit early.

9/10 🏦 **WHY RETAIL TRADERS ALWAYS LOSE**
Most traders buy the first breakout. They get stopped out during the Correction. Their stop-losses FUND the real Continuation move.

The ICC trader watches this happen, then calmly enters on the second push — using the retail trader's money as fuel for their own trade.

10/10 🏆 **THE FULL ICC PAYOFF**
Master all 3 steps:
Indication: Know the direction.
Correction: Wait patiently.
Continuation: Enter the second push. Place your Stop Loss on structure. Target the 1H level.
