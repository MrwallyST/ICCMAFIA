# Day 4: Continuations & The Entry Model

Welcome to Day 4 of the ICC Master Blueprint. Today, everything comes together. We are diving into Step 3 of the ICC Framework: **The Continuation**. If indications tell you the trend, and corrections clear out the amateurs, the continuation is exactly how and where you actually get paid.

## The Grand Finale: What is a Continuation?

The continuation happens right after the correction is over. You are waiting for the market to stop grabbing liquidity, flip back into the original trend, and take price back into your desired direction. 

**The Cycle:** This is the exact rhythm of a profitable market. 
`Indication ➡️ Correction ➡️ Continuation ➡️ Repeat`

The market breaks out, pulls back to shake out weak hands, and then continues the original move.

## The Surgeon Analogy: Multi-Timeframe Mastery

To nail the perfect entry, you have to look at the market like a surgeon.

1. **The 1-Hour Chart (The X-Ray):** You find your overall setup and indication on the 1-Hour or 4-Hour timeframe. This tells you the massive, overall direction.
2. **The 5-Minute Chart (The Scalpel):** But to get a precise entry, you have to drop down to the 15-minute or 5-minute chart. If a patient says they have a headache, a doctor goes deep inside with an X-ray to see what is really going on. As a trader, you go deep inside the 1-Hour trend using the 5-minute chart to pinpoint exactly when the correction is over and the real move is starting.

## The Execution: How to Place the Trade

Here is the blueprint for executing the perfect continuation trade:

### The Second Time Around
You **never** trade the first breakout. You wait for the market to drop, form a correction, and then you enter the second time price pushes past your key level on the 5-minute chart.

### Stop Loss & Take Profit Placement
- **Stop Loss:** Once you enter, you place your Stop Loss safely above or below the previous peak (the lower high or higher low) created during the correction.
- **Take Profit:** Your Take Profit target is the massive 1-Hour level where the original indication started—aiming to create a brand new low or high.
