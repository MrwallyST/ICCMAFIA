# Continuations & The Entry Model

Step 1 (Indication) tells you where the market is going. Step 2 (Correction) traps the amateurs. But Step 3—**The Continuation**—is exactly how and where you actually get paid. 

In Day 4 of the ICC Master Blueprint, we dissect the Grand Finale of the trade cycle.

## The Surgeon Analogy

To nail the perfect entry, you must look at the market like a surgeon:

*   **The 1-Hour Chart (The X-Ray):** This gives you the massive, overall direction. You use the X-Ray to find the symptom (your Indication). 
*   **The 5-Minute Chart (The Scalpel):** To get a precise entry, you must drop down to the 15-minute or 5-minute chart. As a trader, you go deep inside the 1-Hour trend to pinpoint *exactly* when the correction is over and the real move is starting.

## The Second Time Around

Most traders lose money because they jump at the first sign of a breakout. The golden rule of the Continuation is to **never trade the first breakout.** 

Let the market drop. Let it form a correction. Then, enter the *second* time price pushes past your key level on the 5-minute chart. This confirms that the market has finished grabbing liquidity and is ready to move safely.

## Placing Stops & Targets

Once you execute your continuation entry:
*   **Stop Loss:** Place it safely above or below the previous peak (the lower high or higher low) created during the correction. This structure protects you.
*   **Take Profit:** Aim for the massive 1-Hour level where the original indication started, anticipating the creation of a brand new high or low.

Master the rhythm: Indication, Correction, Continuation, Repeat.
