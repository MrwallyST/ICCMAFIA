## 2. Finding Your Swing Points: Stripping the Noise
To see the market for what it truly is, you must clear the "chaos." This starts by removing every indicator and focusing on recent price action—specifically the last 1 to 3 days or sessions. While these concepts apply across the board, Sci’s technical preference is to use the **1-hour timeframe** for marking these up to maintain the best balance of detail and authority.

When marking these levels, we prioritize **body closes** over wicks. A body close represents a "closed deal" and a solidified commitment from the market, whereas a wick is simply a temporary probe that failed to hold.

> "A swing high or low isn't about memorizing some complex candle pattern; it's that undeniable obvious visual point where price basically hit a brick wall and reversed. It’s the specific peak or valley where price drastically changed direction because strong buyers or sellers were sitting exactly at that level waiting to pounce." — **Trades by Sci**

## 3. The Hierarchy of Timeframe Authority
A Senior Analyst understands that market structure is a product of volume. A 4-hour trend is harder to break than a 15-minute fluctuation because a 4-hour structure requires significantly more money and institutional volume to shift. Therefore, the Higher Time Frame (HTF) is the ultimate authority. If a 1-hour chart looks bearish but the 4-hour is in a massive uptrend, the 1-hour move is likely just a healthy pullback. We always defer to the higher level in the hierarchy.

### Timeframe Power Dynamics
| Timeframe | Role in Strategy | Authority Level |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **4-Hour** | Determines Macro Strength & Trend | **Ultimate Authority:** Overpowers all lower noise. |
| **1-Hour** | Structural Development & Markup | **Moderate Authority:** Sci's preferred level for swing points. |
| **30-Minute** | Intermediate Trend Confirmation | **Low Authority:** Bridges the gap between 1H and 15m. |
| **15-Minute** | Execution & Entry Verification | **Lowest Authority:** Must align with the 4H/1H to be valid. |

## 4. The Three-Step Timeframe Alignment Process
Trading without alignment is gambling. We follow a strict, non-negotiable process to ensure the market "Lamborghini" has a clear path.

*   **Step 0: Identify the No Trade Zone.** Before looking for entries, define the space between your swing high and swing low. If price is bouncing between these levels without a breakout, you are in a No Trade Zone. Sit on your hands.
*   **Step 1: Identify the 4-Hour Overarching Trend.** Determine the macro direction. Is the "big money" buying or selling?
*   **Step 2: Wait for 1-Hour Structural Development.** Look for an **Indication**—a break of a swing high or low that provides evidence of a trend shift. Do not trade the Indication itself; wait for the market to correct and grab liquidity.
*   **Step 3: Verify 15-Minute Bias Alignment.** Drop to the 15-minute chart. This is your **"Green Light."** The 15-minute must be building structure (higher highs/lows for buys) that matches the 1-hour and 4-hour direction.

## 5. Market Momentum: The Lamborghini and the Highway
Price movement is best visualized as a Lamborghini speeding down a highway. When buyers or sellers break past a major structural point with high volume, they "punch the gas." 

*   **The Open Highway:** When price breaks into an area with no historical sellers (in a buy) or buyers (in a sell), it accelerates rapidly because there is no resistance.
*   **The "Slow Nancies":** These are the counter-trend sellers or buyers sitting in established zones. They are the "traffic" of the market. If the aligned HTF momentum is strong enough, these Slow Nancies have to get the heck out of the way or get run over until the buyers/sellers eventually run out of gas.
*   **Outcomes:**
    *   **Acceleration:** Price punches through the traffic and hits the open road.
    *   **Stalling:** Price hits a heavy zone of Slow Nancies and begins to stall or reverse, signifying the move is out of gas.

## 6. The Psychology of Positioning vs. FOMO
The professional's superpower is the ability to wait. Most beginners are "button-clickers" who chase the first leg of a move because they fear missing out (FOMO). The professional waits for the **"second time around"**—the continuation after the correction. This eliminates greed, revenge trading, and false hope by forcing the trader to rely on logical evidence rather than impulsive emotion.

### Professional Positioning Requirements
*   [ ] **Structural Clarity:** Have I stripped the indicators and marked my body closes on the 1-hour chart?
*   [ ] **HTF Alignment:** Are the 4-hour and 1-hour biases moving in the same direction?
*   [ ] **The Green Light:** Is the 15-minute chart showing the same structural bias as the HTF?
*   [ ] **Positioning vs. Chasing:** Am I entering on the "second leg" after a correction, or am I FOMOing into the first Indication (the breakout)?
*   [ ] **Logical Basis:** Is this trade based on Sci’s alignment rules or a "gut feeling"?
