### 2. Finding Your Visual Anchors (Swing Points)
The first rule of a clean chart? Stop looking at the past. You don't need levels from three months ago. Follow the **1 to 3 Sessions Rule**: only look at the last one to three trading sessions (specifically London and New York, where the real volume lives).

Turn off every single indicator. We are looking for "brick walls"—undeniable points where price hit a peak or valley and did a 180-degree turn. 

**The Anatomy of a True Swing Point**
*   **The "Closed Deal" Rule:** We mark **Body Closes**, not wicks. A body close is a "closed deal" where the market committed to a price. A wick is just a "visit"—it doesn’t count as a respected level.
*   **Undeniable Visibility:** If you have to squint or use a magnifying glass to find it, it’s not a swing point. It should hit you in the face.
*   **The Brick Wall:** A specific peak where price hit a wall and reversed with strength. This shows you exactly where the big players—the institutional money—are sitting and waiting to pounce.
*   **Zero Noise:** We forbid "Double Bottoms" or complex patterns. If the structure is clean, the "Slow Nancies" (weak traders) will be obvious, and the path for the pros will be clear.

### 3. The Ultimate Authority: Higher Time Frame (HTF) Respect
In this game, there is a clear **Hierarchy of Respect**: the 4-hour (4H) is the boss, the 1-hour (1H) is the manager, and the 15-minute (15M) is the worker. 

Why? Because it takes massive volume and millions of dollars to move a 4H candle. Lower time frames are just "noise" and temporary fluctuations. You might see a "trend reversal" on the 15M and panic, but if you zoom out to the 4H, you’ll see it’s just a healthy pullback in a massive uptrend.

**The Golden Command:** Whenever you have conflicting signals, you must defer to the higher time frame. If the 4H is bullish, we do not look for sells, period. Respect the macro strength or get crushed by it.

### 4. The ICC Strategy: A Three-Step Execution Roadmap
The ICC method ensures you aren't just clicking buttons; you are positioning yourself with the trend.

*   **I - Indication (The Blueprint):** This is a visual cue, **not** an entry. An indication happens when price punches through a visual swing high or low. It’s the market giving you a "blueprint" or a clue of where it wants to go. 
*   **The No Trade Zone:** Between your swing high and swing low, you are in the "No Trade Zone." You sit on your hands. We do nothing until an Indication breaks the boundary and shows us the trend is starting.
*   **C - Correction (Shaking the Coke Bottle):** After the burst, the market needs to "grab its wallet" before the trip. This is a liquidity grab designed to shake out the FOMO traders and the "Slow Nancies" who jumped in on the breakout. Think of it like shaking a Coke bottle—the pressure builds as price pulls back, grabbing money from stop losses. This is the phase for extreme patience.
*   **C - Continuation (Punching the Gas):** This is the entry. We drop to the 15M chart and wait for the structure to "flip." If we are buying, we wait for the 15M to break a **lower high** and start making higher highs. This confirms the "Coke bottle" is ready to pop and the 15M is now aligned with the 1H and 4H bias.

**The ICC Workflow**

| Stage | Action | Primary Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Indication** | **Identify Blueprint** | Shows the trend's intended direction and targets. |
| **Correction** | **Shaking the Bottle** | Wipes out greedy FOMO traders and collects liquidity. |
| **Continuation** | **Punch the Gas** | Confirms alignment and triggers the high-probability entry. |

### 5. Market Momentum: The "Lamborghini" Analogy
Market momentum is like a **Lamborghini speeding down an empty highway**. When buyers punch through a resistance level and there’s no "traffic" (historical sellers) in the way, they "punch the gas." 

As the Lambo rolls down the hill, it gathers "water" (momentum/volume). It uses that weight to smash through the next level. "Traffic" represents the "Slow Nancies" or weak sellers who try to stand in the way. If the 4H and 1H are aligned, those sellers have to get the heck out of the way or get run over.

**The Directive:** You either hop in the Lamborghini for the ride or you stay off the highway completely. Never stand in front of aligned momentum.
