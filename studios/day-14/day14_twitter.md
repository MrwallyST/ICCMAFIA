## 2. Core Methodology: Identifying Swing Points and Clearing the Noise
To trade like a professional, you must first stop looking at your charts like an amateur. Strip away every indicator. If your chart looks like a Jackson Pollock painting with a million daily and weekly levels, you’ve already lost.

### The Rules of the Markup
*   **The 3-Session Rule:** Only mark up the last 1 to 3 sessions (London and New York). Anything further back is irrelevant to today’s liquidity.
*   **Body Closes Over Wicks:** This is a non-negotiable technical rule. A body close is a "closed deal." A wick is just a "hey, we’ve been here but didn't successfully stay." We mark our levels based on where the deals were closed.
*   **The "Brick Wall" Effect:** A Swing High or Swing Low is an undeniable visual peak or valley where price hit a literal brick wall and reversed. It is a drastic change in direction driven by strong buyers or sellers waiting to pounce. Trust your eyes, not a complex candle pattern.

## 3. The "No Trade Zone" and the ICC Execution Model
Before you even think about an entry, you must identify the **No Trade Zone**. This is the space between a swing high and a swing low where no indication has occurred. Trading inside this zone is, quite frankly, stupid. It shows a complete lack of discipline. You sit on your hands until the market provides a blueprint.

### The ICC Sequence
1.  **Indication:** Price breaks a swing high or low with a body close. This is **evidence**, not an entry. It’s the starting point that answers "where is price headed?" If you trade the breakout (the Indication), you will get liquidated. You'll be the one in my DMs asking for $50 because you got trapped.
2.  **Correction:** The "Wave and Water" phase. After a new high/low is made, the market pulls back to grab liquidity (water) to fuel the next move. This phase shakes out the FOMO traders. Price is always chasing an **Incentive**—a dream of a higher high or lower low. The correction is the market gathering the "gas" to chase that dream.
3.  **Continuation:** This is where we actually enter. We wait for the 15-minute structure to flip back into alignment with the Indication. We only enter when the "Slow Nancies" are out of the way and the trend resumes its original direction.

## 5. Market Momentum and "The Lamborghini" Metaphor

*   **The Lamborghini:** When price clears a zone and has no historical sellers blocking the path, it "punches the gas." It will speed until it hits a new level of sellers.
*   **Slow Nancies:** These are the weak sellers or buyers standing in the middle of the highway. When a 4H Lamborghini is at full throttle, these "Slow Nancies" get the heck out of the way or they get crushed. 
*   **The Rule:** You either hop in the Lamborghini for the ride or stay off the highway completely. Never try to "predict" a reversal in front of aligned momentum.
