Time Frame Alignment Rules,A systematic three-step process to step down through time frames to ensure the micro-bias matches the macro-structure before entry.,1. Identify 4-hour trend; 2. Wait for 1-hour structure to match; 3. Use 15-minute for execution alignment. These steps are strict and non-negotiable.,"If the 1-hour is bullish, the 15-minute must also be building bullish structure (higher highs/lows) before entering the trade.","[1, 2]"
Higher Time Frame (HTF) Respect,The principle that the market structure of a larger time frame (macro) always overpowers the temporary fluctuations of a lower time frame (micro).,The 4-hour is stronger than the 1-hour; the 1-hour is stronger than the 30-minute. Always defer to the HTF when signals conflict.,"A 1-hour chart might look like a panic reversal, but zooming out to the 4-hour shows it is just a healthy pullback in a massive trend.","[1, 3, 4]"
Swing Points (Highs and Lows),"Undeniable visual points on a chart where price hits a ""brick wall"" and reverses, forming peaks or valleys where strong buyers or sellers were waiting.",Clear out chaos by stripping away indicators; only look at the last 1 to 3 days or sessions. Trust your eyes to spot the drastic visual shifts.,Visually pinpointing a specific peak where price drastically changed direction after a bullish push because sellers were sitting at that level.,"[1, 5]"
Indication,"The first step of the ICC strategy where price breaks a significant swing high or swing low, providing evidence of where volume is heading.","Do not trade the indication itself; it is a ""breakout"" used only for information or direction. It signals that price has the potential to reach a certain level.","Price breaks above a level where sellers were previously sitting, indicating that buyers are now taking control.","[5, 6]"
Market Momentum & Traffic,A concept describing how price accelerates in open space (no resistance) and only slows down when it hits established zones of opposing orders.,"When buyers break resistance with no historical sellers in the way, they ""punch the gas."" You either join the momentum or stay out of the way.",The Lamborghini analogy: A car speeding down an empty highway (momentum) until it hits a zone of sellers (traffic) causing it to stall.,"[1, 4]"
No Trade Zone,An area of price action between an established high and low where there is no clear indication of direction.,Never place a trade when price is between a swing high and swing low. This requires the discipline to wait for a clear directional break.,"Price is bouncing between a gray resistance box and a support floor without breaking either; the trader ""sits on their hands.""","[3, 5]"
Psychology of Positioning,The mental discipline of waiting for high-probability setups where all time frames align rather than forcing trades due to FOMO.,It is acceptable to miss a trade to gain extra confirmation. True professionals prioritize logic and alignment over impulsive emotion.,"Missing a trade because the 15-minute hasn't aligned with the 4-hour yet; viewing the act of waiting as a ""superpower"" of discipline.","[1, 4, 7]"
