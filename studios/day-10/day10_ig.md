**Day 10: How to Mark Up Charts**

Your chart looks like a laser tag arena. 🔫🛑 

Too many lines. Too much noise. Analysis paralysis. 

Step 1: Delete everything. 🧹
Step 2: Use the "1 to 3 Session Rule". You only need to look back at the last 1-3 trading sessions to find the real swing highs and lows. ⏱️
Step 3: The space between your high and low is the NO TRADE ZONE. Sit on your hands until price breaks out and gives you an Indication! 🙅‍♂️

Keep your charts clean, trade with common sense, and wait for session volume.
