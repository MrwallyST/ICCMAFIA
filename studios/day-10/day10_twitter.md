**Day 10: The "1 to 3 Session Rule" for Chart Markups 🧵👇**

1/ If your chart has 15 different lines from the Daily, 4H, and 1H timeframes... you are doing it wrong. You are causing your own analysis paralysis. Step one today: Delete everything. Clear the chart. 🧹

2/ Introduce the "1 to 3 Session Rule." You do not need to look back weeks to find levels. The most important institutional liquidity sits in the last 1 to 3 trading sessions. Mark the highest high and the lowest low from that recent window. ⏱️

3/ Use common sense to find these swings. If price pushed up aggressively, stopped dead in its tracks, and reversed... what does that mean? It means sellers are sitting right there. That's your Swing High. 🧠

4/ The space between your Swing High and Swing Low is the NO TRADE ZONE. Do not place a trade here. You sit on your hands and wait. You must have the discipline to do nothing until the market moves. 🙅‍♂️

5/ When price breaks out of the No Trade Zone, that is your Indication. DO NOT trade the breakout! Wait for the Correction and session volume (London/NY) to confirm the Continuation. Watch the full Day 10 class on the ICCMAFIA dashboard! 🌐
