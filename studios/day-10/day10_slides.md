# Slide 1: Analysis Paralysis
* **The Problem:** Marking levels from the Daily, 4-Hour, and 1-Hour charts creates a massive mess of horizontal lines.
* **The Symptom:** Hesitation and confusion. Every candle looks like it is hitting resistance.
* **The Fix:** Delete everything. Clear your chart for a fresh start.

---

# Slide 2: The 1 to 3 Session Rule
* **The Rule:** You only need to look at the price action from the most recent 1 to 3 trading sessions.
* **Why?** The most actionable institutional liquidity and volume are found in the immediate past.
* **The Action:** Identify where price pushed aggressively, stopped abruptly, and reversed.

---

# Slide 3: The No Trade Zone
* **Identify Boundaries:** Mark the most recent valid Swing High and Swing Low.
* **The Zone:** The space between these two boundaries is the "No Trade Zone".
* **The Discipline:** You must sit on your hands. Never place a trade while price chops around in this area.

---

# Slide 4: Trading the Breakout (The Right Way)
* **The Indication:** Price breaking out of the No Trade Zone is your Indication. DO NOT trade this breakout.
* **The Correction:** Wait for the market to correct and grab liquidity from early breakout buyers.
* **The Continuation:** Execute the trade only when a major session (like London or NY 9:30 AM) steps in with volume to confirm the direction.
