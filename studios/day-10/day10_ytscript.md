**[1:45] The 1 to 3 Session Rule**
"You do not need to look back a month to find your levels. You only need the last one to three trading sessions. That's it. Turn on your session indicators. Look at the last three sessions. Where did price stop abruptly? That is where the buyers and sellers are."

**[4:00] Finding Swings (Common Sense)**
"If price was pushing up hard, and then just stopped and tanked... what does that tell you? Common sense! It means sellers are sitting right there. Mark that as your Swing High. If you need help, turn on the 'Pivot Points High and Low' indicator to test your eyes."

**[6:30] The No Trade Zone & The Indication**
"Once you mark your high and low, the space in between is the No Trade Zone. You sit on your hands. You do not trade. When price finally breaks out of that zone, that is your Indication. But remember from the ICC Framework: DO NOT TRADE THE INDICATION. It's a breakout trap."
