Key Concept,Definition,Trading Application,Psychological Insight,Example,Source
Indication,A calculated move or breakout where price breaks above or below a swing high or swing low level.,Used as information rather than an entry signal; identifies the starting point of a trend on higher time frames (1H or 4H).,Eliminates the fear of missing out (FOMO) by treating the breakout as a 'signal fire' or evidence rather than a reason to chase.,The 'Coke bottle' explosion: when you shake the bottle and the cap flies off (the breakout).,[1-3]
Correction,A pause or reversal after a big breakout designed to shake out emotional traders and grab liquidity.,Monitored on the 15-minute chart to identify when the 'mess' is being cleaned up before entering the continuation phase.,Reduces greed and panic; teaches the trader to view pullbacks as the market 'showing its hand' rather than a failed trade.,The 'fizz' or 'mess' after a Coke bottle explodes that must be cleaned up before the market continues.,"[1, 4]"
Continuation,The phase where price resumes the real trend after the correction is finished and liquidity has been grabbed.,Entry point where price breaks back above/below the previous level with confirmed market structure alignment.,Promotes patience; the trader shifts focus from 'trading' every move to 'positioning' for the high-probability sustainable trend.,"Person A vs. Person B: Person B waits an extra week to work/save before spending, resulting in a better financial position.","[1, 5, 6]"
Invalidation,"A point where a price level is broken, proving that the current trade idea or positioning was incorrect.","Specific placement of stop-losses at levels where, if hit, the trend's structure is definitively broken.","Removes the 'marriage' to a trade; provides a clear, emotionless exit strategy when the thesis is proven wrong.","Placing a stop-loss under a level where buyers previously said 'Hell no, you can't come lower than this.'","[2, 5]"
Liquidity,A pool of money consisting of stop-losses and pending orders that the market needs to fuel a sustainable move.,The market dips into these 'pools' of money during a correction to gather the cash required for the real trend.,Helps traders stop feeling that stopped-out trades are 'personal' and instead see them as a mechanical part of market cycles.,Retail traders piling in at the top of a move create a 'giant pool of money' for the market to grab.,"[1, 7]"
