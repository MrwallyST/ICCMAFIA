Correction (Market Correction),"Multiple (4h, 1h, 15m)",A move contrary to the main trend characterized by structure that does not break the overall higher time frame trend.,Consists of lower highs and higher lows (temporary counter-trend) within a larger bullish or bearish trend.,"On the 4-hour time frame, price makes a new high; meanwhile, the 1-hour time frame begins to downtrend temporarily.",Wait for the smaller time frame (15-minute) to align back with the higher time frame (4-hour) trend to confirm the correction is over.,[1]
