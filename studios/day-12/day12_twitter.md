# Day 12 Twitter/X Thread — Decision Making & Trading Psychology

---

🧵 Day 12 of the TradesBySci ICC Course just dropped.

This one isn't about entries. It's about the mental framework you need to actually WIN.

Thread 👇

---

1/ The higher timeframe ALWAYS wins.

4-hour beats 1-hour.
1-hour beats 30-minute.

If they're contradicting each other — you follow the bigger picture. No debate.

---

2/ It's okay to miss a trade for more confirmation.

You skip the first higher high.
You wait.
You catch the rest of the trend.

The market doesn't disappear. Patience IS the edge.

---

3/ 3 non-negotiable structure rules:

① Mark body closes — not wicks (closed deals vs. rejected visits)
② To buy → price must break ABOVE a swing high
③ To sell → price must break BELOW a swing low

No exceptions.

---

4/ The Previous Push Rule 🔑

Every swing high was built from somewhere.

Find that origin.

The moment price breaks BELOW that origin? Bullish momentum is officially DEAD.

---

5/ The Open Highway Analogy:

No sellers above = empty road = buyers punch the gas 🚗

Price flies at max speed until it hits the next cluster of sellers (traffic).

If buyers maneuver through? They punch the gas again.

---

6/ The Lamborghini Effect:

If you're a seller and you see buyers coming in at FULL strength — you move out of the way.

The trend continues until buyers run out of gas.

Don't fight strong momentum. Ride it.

---

7/ Stop trading Forex for volatility. 🛑

EUR/USD, GBP/JPY = stable. They move off news.

Trade INVESTMENTS:
- NASDAQ
- Gold  
- Crypto
- S&P 500

People pour money into these EVERY DAY. That's your volatility.

---

8/ Time matters.

New York session. 9:30 AM EST.

That's when the NYSE opens. Banks flood the market.

Volume is the gas pedal — and 9:30 is when someone floors it.

---

9/ The All-Time High Trap (Clothing Drop Analogy) 👟📉

Limited drop → demand high → value up
More drops → fakes flood market → everyone has it
Hype dies → value tanks

SAME THING happens at market all-time highs.

The sign: price slows → breaks below previous low → selling pressure takes over

---

10/ Asset Correlation:

S&P 500 leads NASDAQ leads Dow Jones
Bitcoin leads ALL crypto

BTC stalls → Solana stalls.
S&P drops while NASDAQ tries to rise → sit on your hands. Wait.

Markets always chase a NEW incentive. No incentive = lower probability.

---

11/ The bottom line from Day 12:

The edge isn't in finding the perfect setup.

It's in knowing when NOT to trade.
