# Day 12 FAQ — Decision Making & Trading Psychology

**Q: What happens when the 1-hour and 4-hour charts show opposite directions?**
A: Follow the 4-hour. The higher timeframe always overpowers the lower one. The hierarchy never changes: Daily > 4H > 1H > 30M > 15M > 5M.

**Q: Is it okay to skip a trade entry if I'm not sure?**
A: Yes — 100%. TradesbySci says it's perfectly fine to miss the first higher high or lower low for extra confirmation. You'll catch the rest of the trend. Being picky protects your capital.

**Q: Why should I mark body closes instead of wicks?**
A: Body closes represent where the market actually closed and committed. Wicks show price visited a level but was rejected. Your key structure should be built on confirmed closes, not fleeting touches.

**Q: What is the "Previous Push" rule?**
A: When marking a swing high, look left to find exactly where that bullish momentum originated. That origin level is critical — if price closes below it, the bullish momentum is officially dead.

**Q: What does the Open Highway analogy mean?**
A: When buyers break above the last seller level, there are no sellers in the way — like an empty highway. Buyers push price at full speed until they hit the next cluster of sellers (traffic). Strong buyers make sellers move out of the way (the Lamborghini Effect).

**Q: Why does TradesbySci not trade Forex pairs?**
A: Forex currencies are stable — they move primarily off economic news, not constant investment liquidity. Assets like NASDAQ, Gold, and Crypto have money poured into them every day, creating the volatility needed for day trading.

**Q: When is the best time to trade NASDAQ, Gold, and Crypto?**
A: New York session — 9:30 AM EST. That's when the NYSE opens and banks pour maximum liquidity into the market. Volume drives price action.

**Q: Why should I avoid buying assets at all-time highs?**
A: At all-time highs, investors who are sitting on large gains may liquidate at any moment, causing violent crashes. The "Clothing Drop" analogy explains it — when everyone has the product, the hype dies and value tanks. Watch for price to break below its previous low as the signal.

**Q: How does BTC affect altcoins like Solana?**
A: BTC leads all of crypto, just as the S&P 500 leads NASDAQ and Dow Jones. If BTC has no new incentive (no new high to chase), altcoins will stall too. Always check the leader before trading the follower.

**Q: What is a "new incentive" and why does it matter?**
A: Markets are always chasing a new high or new low. Without a new target to chase, price stalls and probability drops significantly. If there's no new incentive, TradesbySci says to sit on your hands and wait.
