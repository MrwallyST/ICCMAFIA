Welcome to ICCMAFIA-AI. Today is Day 12 — Decision Making and Trading Psychology. This is the most important lesson in the entire curriculum because without the right mental framework, nothing else works.

**[TIMEFRAME AUTHORITY]**
The higher timeframe ALWAYS wins. 4-hour beats 1-hour. 1-hour beats 30-minute. When they contradict — follow the bigger picture. It's also okay to miss a trade for more confirmation. Skip the first higher high. Wait. Catch the rest of the trend.

**[STRUCTURE RULES]**
Three non-negotiables: (1) Mark body closes, not wicks — body closes are closed deals, wicks are rejections. (2) Golden Swing Rules — to buy, price must break above a swing high; to sell, break below a swing low. (3) Previous Push Rule — find where that bullish push originated. If price closes below that origin, bullish momentum is officially dead.

**[OPEN HIGHWAY]**
Buyers above the last seller level = empty highway = punch the gas. Price flies until it hits the next seller cluster (traffic). The Lamborghini Effect: sellers facing full-strength buyers move out of the way. Trend continues until buyers run out of gas. Don't fight strong momentum — ride it.

**[FOREX IS DEAD]**
Stop trading Forex for volatility. EUR/USD, GBP/JPY are stable and move off news. Trade investments: NASDAQ, Gold, Crypto, S&P 500. People pour money into these daily. Trade them at 9:30 AM EST New York session — maximum volume, maximum movement.

**[ALL-TIME HIGH TRAP]**
The clothing drop analogy: limited jeans drop → demand explodes → more drops → fakes flood in → everyone has it → hype dies → value tanks. Same at market all-time highs. Investors up 30% in 3 days can liquidate any moment. The warning sign: price breaks below its previous low — hype is dead, sellers take over.

**[CORRELATION & INCENTIVES]**
S&P 500 leads NASDAQ leads Dow Jones. Bitcoin leads all crypto. If BTC stalls with no new incentive, Solana stalls too. If S&P drops while NASDAQ tries to rise — sit on your hands. Markets always need a new incentive to chase. No new incentive = no trade.
