# Day 12 Quiz: Decision Making & Trading Psychology

## Question 1
You're watching the 1-hour chart, which looks bullish. But the 4-hour chart is showing bearish structure. What should you do?

- [ ] Trade the 1-hour setup — it's more detailed
- [ ] Wait for both charts to agree before entering
- [x] Follow the 4-hour chart — it always overpowers the 1-hour
- [ ] Switch to a 15-minute chart for confirmation

**Hint:** The higher timeframe always wins. The 4-hour will overpower the 1-hour.

## Question 2
When marking key levels on a chart, TradesbySci says you should primarily use:

- [ ] Wick highs and wick lows
- [x] Candle body closes
- [ ] Opening prices
- [ ] The midpoint of large candles

**Hint:** Body closes are "closed deals." Wicks just mean price visited but was rejected.

## Question 3
According to the "Previous Push" rule, what does it mean if price breaks BELOW the origin of a bullish push?

- [ ] Price is about to reverse back up
- [ ] Sellers are temporarily taking over — wait for a correction
- [x] The bullish momentum is officially dead
- [ ] It's a buy signal — price is at a discount

**Hint:** Price breaking below the origin of the previous bullish push means that momentum is lost.

## Question 4
The "Open Highway" analogy describes what happens when:

- [ ] Price approaches a major resistance level
- [x] There are no sellers above the current price level
- [ ] Volume is low during the Asian session
- [ ] Price is consolidating in a tight range

**Hint:** When buyers break above the last seller level, there's nothing in the way. They "punch the gas" like driving an empty highway.

## Question 7
The "Clothing Drop" analogy (limited jeans drop, fakes flood the market, value tanks) is used to explain:

- [ ] Why you should buy breakouts immediately
- [x] How all-time highs lose their hype and why selling pressure builds
- [ ] The correction phase of the ICC cycle
- [ ] Why Forex pairs are more stable than crypto

**Hint:** When everyone has a pair of the jeans (everyone's in the trade), the hype dies and value tanks — just like price at an all-time high.

## Question 8
If BTC has stalled and has no new high to chase, what does TradesbySci say about trading Solana?

- [ ] Solana will move independently — trade it freely
- [x] Solana will likely halt too until BTC makes new movements
- [ ] Solana always moves opposite to BTC
- [ ] Buy Solana because it has its own momentum

## Question 9
According to Day 12, what is the MOST important thing price needs to move in a sustained direction?

- [ ] A chart pattern (head and shoulders, triangle, etc.)
- [ ] A news catalyst from the Federal Reserve
- [x] A new incentive — a new high or new low to chase

## Question 10
If the S&P 500 is going DOWN while the NASDAQ is trying to go UP, TradesbySci says you should:

- [ ] Buy NASDAQ — it's showing strength relative to S&P
- [ ] Short NASDAQ because S&P leads it
- [x] Take it with a grain of salt, sit on your hands, and wait
