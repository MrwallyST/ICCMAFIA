# Day 12 Knowledge Quiz — Decision Making & Trading Psychology

Test your understanding of Day 12 concepts. All answers are derived directly from the TradesbySci ICC Course.

---

**Q1. You're watching the 1-hour chart, which looks bullish. But the 4-hour chart is showing bearish structure. What should you do?**

A) Trade the 1-hour setup — it's more detailed  
B) Wait for both charts to agree before entering  
C) Follow the 4-hour chart — it always overpowers the 1-hour  
D) Switch to a 15-minute chart for confirmation  

✅ **Answer: C** — The higher timeframe always wins. The 4-hour will overpower the 1-hour.

---

**Q2. When marking key levels on a chart, TradesbySci says you should primarily use:**

A) Wick highs and wick lows  
B) Candle body closes  
C) Opening prices  
D) The midpoint of large candles  

✅ **Answer: B** — Body closes are "closed deals." Wicks just mean price visited but was rejected.

---

**Q3. According to the "Previous Push" rule, what does it mean if price breaks BELOW the origin of a bullish push?**

A) Price is about to reverse back up  
B) Sellers are temporarily taking over — wait for a correction  
C) The bullish momentum is officially dead  
D) It's a buy signal — price is at a discount  

✅ **Answer: C** — Price breaking below the origin of the previous bullish push means that momentum is lost.

---

**Q4. The "Open Highway" analogy describes what happens when:**

A) Price approaches a major resistance level  
B) There are no sellers above the current price level  
C) Volume is low during the Asian session  
D) Price is consolidating in a tight range  

✅ **Answer: B** — When buyers break above the last seller level, there's nothing in the way. They "punch the gas" like driving an empty highway.

---

**Q5. TradesbySci recommends trading which of the following asset categories for maximum volatility?**

A) EUR/USD, GBP/JPY, USD/CAD  
B) Forex pairs during London session  
C) NASDAQ, Gold, Crypto, S&P 500  
D) Commodities like oil and wheat  

✅ **Answer: C** — Investments like NASDAQ, Gold, and Crypto have constant liquidity poured into them daily, creating the volatility needed for trading.

---

**Q6. What time does TradesbySci recommend trading USD-based assets like NASDAQ and Gold for maximum volume?**

A) London open — 3:00 AM EST  
B) Asian session — midnight EST  
C) New York Stock Exchange open — 9:30 AM EST  
D) End of day — 4:00 PM EST  

✅ **Answer: C** — The New York session at 9:30 AM EST is when banks pour massive liquidity into the market.

---

**Q7. The "Clothing Drop" analogy (limited jeans drop, fakes flood the market, value tanks) is used to explain:**

A) Why you should buy breakouts immediately  
B) How all-time highs lose their hype and why selling pressure builds  
C) The correction phase of the ICC cycle  
D) Why Forex pairs are more stable than crypto  

✅ **Answer: B** — When everyone has a pair of the jeans (everyone's in the trade), the hype dies and value tanks — just like price at an all-time high.

---

**Q8. If BTC has stalled and has no new high to chase, what does TradesbySci say about trading Solana?**

A) Solana will move independently — trade it freely  
B) Solana will likely halt too until BTC makes new movements  
C) Solana always moves opposite to BTC  
D) Buy Solana because it has its own momentum  

✅ **Answer: B** — BTC is the leader of crypto. If BTC has no new incentive, altcoins like Solana stall with it.

---

**Q9. According to Day 12, what is the MOST important thing price needs to move in a sustained direction?**

A) A chart pattern (head and shoulders, triangle, etc.)  
B) A news catalyst from the Federal Reserve  
C) A new incentive — a new high or new low to chase  
D) Support and resistance levels from 6 months ago  

✅ **Answer: C** — Markets always need a new incentive (new high or new low) to chase. Without one, probability drops significantly.

---

**Q10. If the S&P 500 is going DOWN while the NASDAQ is trying to go UP, TradesbySci says you should:**

A) Buy NASDAQ — it's showing strength relative to S&P  
B) Short NASDAQ because S&P leads it  
C) Take it with a grain of salt, sit on your hands, and wait  
D) Switch to trading Gold instead  

✅ **Answer: C** — Contradicting correlation is a red flag. The right move is to wait for the market to make a clear decision before entering.
