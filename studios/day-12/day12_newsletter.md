### 🏆 The Higher Timeframe Always Wins

When you're on the 1-hour and it looks bullish, but the 4-hour says bearish — you follow the 4-hour. Every time.

The hierarchy: Daily → 4-Hour → 1-Hour → 30-Min → 15-Min → 5-Min

The psychological edge: it's completely okay to miss a trade while waiting for more confirmation. The market will give you another chance. Missing one setup to catch the entire trend is a professional mindset.

---

### 📐 3 Non-Negotiable Structure Rules

1. **Body closes > wicks.** Mark your levels based on where candles *closed*, not where wicks reached. Closed = confirmed. Wick = visited and rejected.

2. **Golden Swing Rules.** To buy, price must break ABOVE a swing high. To sell, price must break BELOW a swing low. No anticipating. No exceptions.

3. **Previous Push Rule.** When marking a swing high, find where that bullish push *originated*. If price closes below that origin? Bullish momentum is officially dead.

---

### 🚗 The Open Highway (Momentum)

Once buyers break above the last seller level — there's nothing in the way. They punch the gas like an empty highway.

Price flies to the next cluster of sellers. If buyers maneuver through that traffic — they punch the gas again.

The Lamborghini Effect: sellers at a level who see buyers coming in at full force will move out of the way. The trend continues until buyers run out of gas. Don't fight strong momentum — ride it.

---

### 📈 Trade Investments, Not Forex

Forex pairs are stable. They move primarily off economic news and don't carry the daily investment liquidity you need.

Focus on: **NASDAQ, Gold, Crypto, S&P 500, Dow Jones** — assets that have real money poured into them every single day.

And trade them during **New York session at 9:30 AM EST** — when the NYSE opens and banks flood the market with maximum liquidity. Volume = price movement.

---

### 👟 The All-Time High Trap

The clothing drop analogy is gold (pun intended):

A limited brand drops 10 pairs of jeans → demand explodes → price skyrockets → more drops come → fakes flood in → everyone has a pair → hype dies → value tanks.

At market all-time highs? Same cycle. An investor sitting on a 30% gain in 3 days might liquidate tomorrow. You don't know when. And when they do, price crashes.

The warning sign: price slows at the all-time high → breaks below its previous low → the hype is over.

---

### 🔗 Asset Correlation & Incentives

- S&P 500 leads NASDAQ leads Dow Jones
- Bitcoin leads all crypto

If BTC stalls with no new incentive (no new high to chase), Solana stalls too. If the S&P is dropping while NASDAQ is trying to go up — sit on your hands and wait for clarity.

Markets always chase a new high or new low. No new incentive = lower probability trade.
