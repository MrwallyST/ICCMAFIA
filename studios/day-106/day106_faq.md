### 2. The Multi-Timeframe Technical Framework
As your mentor, I demand a "Clean Chart" philosophy. Strip away the indicators that clutter your vision. We are price-action purists. To find the truth in the market, you only need three timeframes, used in strict correlation.

**The "Bread and Butter" Timeframes:**
*   **Daily Timeframe (The Anchor)**
*   **4-Hour Timeframe**
*   **1-Hour Timeframe**

**The Law of Correlation**
The **Daily timeframe is your anchor**. If the Daily level does not provide a clear bias, the 4-hour and 1-hour timeframes are irrelevant. An entry is only valid when all three timeframes align. If they do not correlate, you do not have a trade.

### 3. Market Structure and Bias Identification
Establishing bias is a systematic process. It is not about "feeling"; it is about what you see. 

**Step-by-Step Bias Establishment:**
1.  **Identify Current Price:** Mark the exact level where the market is trading now.
2.  **Locate the Previous High:** Move your finger up from current price until you hit the last significant high.
3.  **Locate the Previous Low:** Move your finger down from current price until you hit the last significant low.

**The Significance of Level Breakage**
Bias is confirmed when price breaks and stays under a structural level. We look for **Superior Seller Strength**. In our current Gold analysis, the daily level that was recently broken is historically significant—it initiated **two separate major uptrends** in the past. Because sellers were strong enough to break a level that previously held such massive buying pressure, we know the sellers are now in complete control.

### 5. Risk Management and Structural Invalidation
Risk management is governed by the rigid "Rules of Structure": In a downtrend, the market must maintain Lower Highs and Lower Lows.

**Stop-Loss and Invalidation Logic**
Your stop-loss is placed at the **Invalidation Level** (the previous Lower High). 
*   **Retests are acceptable:** Price can move back into the level to retest without invalidating the trade.
*   **Breaks are terminal:** The moment price breaks above the Lower High, the structural integrity is shattered. The sellers' plan is "gone," and you must exit immediately.

> **The Professional Standard:** The market does not want to backtrack once a structural sell-off has begun. It does not want to "go back into the red." If price backtracks past the Lower High, the selling pressure has failed.
