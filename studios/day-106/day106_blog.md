### Technical Foundation: The Power of Timeframe Correlation

To find these setups, you must master the "Bread and Butter" timeframes: the Daily, 4-hour, and 1-hour. If these three do not correlate, there is no trade. Your bias is established on the Daily, and the lower timeframes must fall in line before you execute.

**Step-by-Step Bias Identification:**
1.  **Find the current highest point:** Identify the peak relative to where price is currently sitting to establish your range.
2.  **Identify the last high and low:** Locate the most recent structural points that dictate the immediate market direction.
3.  **Observe key level breaches:** Watch for price to break under or above these structural levels. This is the market telling you who is in control.

### Defining Invalidation: The Logic of Stop-Losses

A stop-loss is not a failure; it is a signal that the "Sellers' Plan" has changed. In a downtrend, the market follows a strict structure of Lower Highs and Lower Lows. 

Sellers who enter at a Lower High have a specific plan: they want to push the price down to a new low. They do not want to backtrack or let the market "go back in red" on their positions. Therefore, the Lower High is your line in the sand.

**The Invalidation Rule:**
If the price breaks back above a Lower High, the sellers' plan has failed. The setup is dead. Do not argue with the chart.

**Command the Trade:**
Treat every level as objective data. Stop fearing the answers the market is providing. Stop psyching yourself out when the chart is communicating exactly what it intends to do.
