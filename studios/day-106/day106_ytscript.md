### 4. Technical Framework: The Triple Timeframe Method
To establish a valid trade bias, three specific timeframes must correlate. This hierarchy is the foundation of the strategy. **If the 4-hour or 1-hour charts contradict the Daily bias, the trade is discarded.**

1.  **The Daily Chart:** Used to identify the overall market bias and the highest/lowest points of recent price action.
2.  **The 4-Hour Chart:** Used to confirm the structure (Lower Highs/Lower Lows) and ensure the mid-term trend aligns with the Daily.
3.  **The 1-Hour Chart:** Used for fine-tuning the entry and ensuring all three timeframes are communicating the same directional intent.

### 5. Market Structure & Bias Identification

#### Current Price Analysis
The process begins by identifying the highest point of the market relative to the current price. By locating the most recent historical high and low, we determine the boundaries of the current environment.

#### Historical Level Breakage
Bias is determined by "breakage" levels. In the current Gold setup, the sell bias is exceptionally strong because the price broke below a critical Daily level that previously **supported an entire daily uptrend**. Because sellers were strong enough to dismantle a level that once initiated a major upward move, it proves that sellers are currently way stronger than buyers.

#### Liquidity Grabs vs. Corrections
It is vital to distinguish between a trend reversal and a simple correction. A price movement against the bias is often a "correction" or a "liquidity grab." For example, a previous attempt at a sell resulted in a loss when price moved higher; however, once price returned below the breakage level, the original sell thesis was reactivated. The temporary move upward was a correction, not a change in the fundamental bearish bias.

### 7. Risk Management and Invalidation Rules
A trade is only valid as long as market structure remains intact. In a downtrend, price must strictly adhere to the rules of Lower Highs and Lower Lows.

> **Warning: Invalidation Criteria**
> The "Invalidation Point" is the most recent Lower High on the 4-Hour timeframe. If price **backtracks** above this level, the "seller's plan is gone." Professionally speaking, sellers do not want to see price move back **"into the red"** above the point where they initiated their heavy downward pressure.

### 8. Psychological Resilience: Market Communication
Professional trading is a matter of response, not reaction. The market provides all necessary answers; the trader’s job is to listen without the interference of ego.

1.  **Market Communication:** View the charts as a dialogue. The market "tells" you where your entry and exit need to be; you simply need to listen to what is being communicated.
2.  **Responding vs. Reacting:** Reacting is driven by fear, greed, or the money on the line. Responding is a calculated action based on the structural answers the market has already provided.
3.  **Ego Management:** Avoid "psyching yourself out" or fearing a loss. If the probability is high and the market is under a key level, take the trade. **"I would rather take the loss than just fear myself out."** Accept the loss as part of the process and wait for the market to communicate the next opportunity.
