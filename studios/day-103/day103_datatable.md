ICC Framework,ICC,"A three-stage market structure process used to identify and trade trends: Indication, Correction, and Continuation.","Involves price establishing a new peak or trough, a pullback that maintains structural integrity, and a final move to complete the cycle.","A rinse-and-repeat cycle used by swing traders; requires alignment across multiple time frames (e.g., 1-hour and 4-hour).",Primary method for tracking trend momentum in markets like BTC and Gold.,[1]
Indication,I,The first stage of a trend cycle where price makes a new high or a new low in the market.,"Price breaks above a previous swing high or below a swing low; establishes the target direction (e.g., a move from \$69,000 to \$73,000).",The Indication level (the previous high/low that was broken) serves as the primary entry level once price returns to it during a correction.,"BTC breaking above \$71,300 or breaking past lower highs on the 4-hour chart to signal bullishness.",[1]
Correction,C,The second stage consisting of a temporary pullback in the opposite direction of the indication.,Must create a Higher Low (HL) for uptrends or a Lower High (LH) for downtrends without breaking the origin of the move.,Price must stay composed above/below key indication levels; used to regain momentum before the next directional push.,BTC price making lower highs and lower lows temporarily after a new high before establishing a higher low.,[1]
Continuation,C,The final stage where price finishes its intended move following the correction phase.,Results in the creation of a new Higher High (HH) in uptrends or a new Lower Low (LL) in downtrends.,Enter when price moves back above/below the indication level; target the previous extreme or the creation of a new high/low.,"BTC moving from a higher low momentum to target new highs above \$73,000.",[1]
Uptrend Requirements,Not in source,Specific structural sequence required to confirm a bullish market direction.,Consists of Higher Highs (HH) and Higher Lows (HL) exclusively.,"Wait for a new high (Indication), then a higher low (Correction) that fails to breach the previous low before seeking entry.","BTC 4-hour structure showing a low, a higher low, and a break of a swing high.",[1]
Downtrend Requirements,Not in source,Specific structural sequence required to confirm a bearish market direction.,Consists of Lower Lows (LL) and Lower Highs (LH) exclusively.,Identify price making new lows followed by pullbacks (Corrections) that stay below previous highs.,Inverse of the BTC bullish setup; relevant when price breaks below established support levels.,[1]
Risk Management / Invalidation,Not in source,Criteria for determining when a trend setup is no longer valid.,Trend is invalidated if price breaks below the previous Higher Low (in an uptrend) or above the previous Lower High (in a downtrend).,"Execute stop loss or exit if price breaks critical structural levels (e.g., breaking below established 4-hour support).","In Gold markets, the trend remains bullish as long as price does not break below the established HL levels on the 4-hour chart.",[1]
