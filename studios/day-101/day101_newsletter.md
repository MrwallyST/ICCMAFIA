### 2. Market Direction Basics
Don’t overcomplicate this. There are only two directions the market moves. If you can't identify these, you have no business putting money on the line.

| Market Direction | Definition |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Uptrend** | Price is moving up. Period. |
| **Downtrend** | Price is moving down. Period. |

*Note: These two directions constitute the entirety of market structure. Everything else is just noise.*

### 3. The Higher Time Frame (HTF) Standard

To be a professional, you only need to focus on three time frames (honestly, I don't even need all of these, but they are the standard):
*   **1 Hour (1H)**
*   **4 Hour (4H)**
*   **Daily (1D)**

### 4. Anatomy of an Uptrend (Buy Logic)
An uptrend is a specific sequence of movements. You are looking for:

*   **Higher High (HH):** A peak that breaks above the previous peak.
*   **Higher Low (HL):** A trough that stays higher than the previous trough.

> **CRUCIAL LOGIC: THE COMPARISON RULE**
> You do not have a trend if price is sitting in a range. You **cannot** call something a Higher High or a Higher Low until you have something to compare it to. No comparison = No structure. Price must move definitively above a previous high to confirm a new HH.

**Trade Execution:** We aren't guessing. **Buy trades are found at the Higher Lows (HL).**

### 5. Anatomy of a Downtrend (Sell Logic)
A downtrend is the inverse of the uptrend logic. 

*   **Lower High (LH):** A peak that fails to reach the height of the previous peak.
*   **Lower Low (LL):** A trough that drops below the previous trough.

**Visual Identification:** A Lower High is the highest point reached before the price starts to sell off heavily and breaks the previous low.

**Trade Execution:** **Sell trades (entries) are found at the Lower Highs (LH).**

### 6. The Golden Rules of Sequence
This is the "main rule." Read this until it is reflexive. This is how you make money.

> **THE BREAD AND BERRY LOGIC:**
> 
> **Uptrend Chain:**
> *   If Price makes a **HL**, it is looking to make a **HH**.
> *   If Price makes a **HH**, it is looking to make a **HL**.
> 
> **Downtrend Chain:**
> *   If Price makes a **LH**, it is looking to make a **LL**.
> *   If Price makes a **LL**, it is looking to make a **LH**.

### 7. Identifying Structural Points on a Live Chart
To find your H and L points, look for the **Shift Identification**. 
*   **Highs:** The highest point reached where green candles shift heavily into a red sell-off. 
*   **Lows:** The lowest point reached where red candles shift into a strong, aggressive buy movement.

**The Range Warning:**
If price is stuck inside a horizontal range, there is no structure. There are no HHs, HLs, LHs, or LLs. If there’s no breakout, there’s no trade. Stay out.

**Pro Tip: Using Indicators**
*   If you're struggling to see it, use the **"Pivot Point High and Low"** indicator.
*   **Setting:** Set the value to **"5"** to force it to identify every significant high and low.
*   **The Caveat:** Indicators can be "a little eh" sometimes. Use them as training wheels, but your goal is to train your eye to see the shifts manually.
