## 2. The Foundation of Market Structure
There are only two directions price can move: **Uptrend** or **Downtrend**. Understanding this is your "cheat code." While others are losing money guessing, you will be reading the market’s DNA. | Trend Type | Simplified Definition |
| :--- | :--- |

| **Downtrend** | A consistent series of **Lower Highs (LH)** and **Lower Lows (LL)**. |

## 4. Anatomy of an Uptrend (Buying Mechanics)
An uptrend is a cycle of growth. To trade it, you must identify **Higher Highs (HH)** and **Higher Lows (HL)**.

*   **The Comparison Rule & The Range:** You cannot identify a HH or a HL in a vacuum. You must have an anchor point to compare it to. If price is bouncing between an established High and Low without breaking out, **you are in a Range.** There is no structure here, and there is no trade. You only have a HH when price physically breaks and closes above the previous High.
*   **Identification:** Look for a strong push up (HH), followed by a pullback that stays above the previous Low (HL).

> **MENTOR’S TIP:** If you are looking for **BUY** trades, your only point of entry is the **HL (Higher Low)**. Never buy at the peak of a HH; wait for the market to come to you at the HL.

## 5. Anatomy of a Downtrend (Selling Mechanics)
A downtrend is a cycle of decline characterized by **Lower Highs (LH)** and **Lower Lows (LL)**.

*   **Lower Low (LL):** Confirmed when price breaks and closes below the previous established floor.
*   **Lower High (LH):** Confirmed when price pulls back up after a drop but fails to exceed the previous high, followed by a heavy rejection.

> **MENTOR’S TIP:** If you are looking for **SELL** trades, your only point of entry is the **LH (Lower High)**. This is where the smart money re-enters the move.

## 6. The Golden Rules of Trend Continuity
This is the "bread and butter." This logic repeats over and over again like a machine. If you memorize these four lines, you can predict the market's next move with high probability.

> **THE CONTINUITY LOGIC**
> *   If Price makes a **Higher Low (HL)**, it is looking to make a **Higher High (HH)**.
> *   If Price makes a **Higher High (HH)**, it is looking to make a **Higher Low (HL)**.
> *   If Price makes a **Lower High (LH)**, it is looking to make a **Lower Low (LL)**.
> *   If Price makes a **Lower Low (LL)**, it is looking to make a **Lower High (LH)**.

A "trend" is simply this pattern repeating until a Higher Time Frame level forces a change.
