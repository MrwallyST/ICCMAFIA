### 3. Directional Analysis: Uptrends vs. Downtrends
Price only moves in two directions. To be a profitable trader, you must identify which way the momentum is swinging by tracking specific price sequences.

*   **Uptrends:** Defined by a sequence of Higher Highs (HH) and Higher Lows (HL).
*   **Downtrends:** Defined by a sequence of Lower Highs (LH) and Lower Lows (LL).

**Trend Identification Symbols**
| Full Term | Shorthand Initial |
| :--- | :--- |
| High | H |
| Low | L |
| Higher High | HH |
| Higher Low | HL |
| Lower High | LH |
| Lower Low | LL |

### 4. The "Golden Rules" of Price Action Sequences
These rules are your "Bread and Butter." This is the repeat pattern that makes the money. When you see one part of the sequence, the next move becomes statistically predictable.

**The Trend Continuation Logic:**
*   **If** price makes a Higher Low (HL) -> **Then** it is expected to make a Higher High (HH).
*   **If** price makes a Higher High (HH) -> **Then** it is expected to make a Higher Low (HL).
*   **If** price makes a Lower High (LH) -> **Then** it is expected to make a Lower Low (LL).
*   **If** price makes a Lower Low (LL) -> **Then** it is expected to make a Lower High (LH).

**The Timeframe Caveat**
While this logic is universal, the **1H, 4H, and Daily levels are the true "Cheat Codes."** The strength of these rules is directly proportional to the timeframe. On small timeframes, the market does not respect these rules; on HTF, they are the law.

### 5. Operationalizing the Trend: Identification and Entry Logic
You cannot trade what you haven't identified. Before you even think about an entry, you must establish a baseline.

**The "First Mission": Finding the High and Low**
The moment you open a chart, your mission is to find the initial **High (H)** and **Low (L)**. 
*   **The Visual Shift:** Don't guess. A High is the highest point reached during a bullish move before a **whole shift** occurs, where green candles turn into a sequence of red candles. A Low is the lowest point before red candles shift back to green.
*   **The Range Prerequisite:** If price is stuck between the initial H and L, it is in a "range." **You are forbidden from labeling a trend until the initial range is broken.** You cannot have a "Higher High" if you have nothing to compare it to. A trend only begins once the price breaks out of the initial H/L boundaries.

**Entry Logic**
We don't chase price; we wait for the structure to come to us:
*   **Buy Trades:** Entries are sourced exclusively from **Higher Lows (HL)**.
*   **Sell Trades:** Entries are sourced exclusively from **Lower Highs (LH)**.

**Technical Aids for Visualization**
Go to the "Indicators" tab and search for **"Pivot Point High and Low."** 
*   **Optimization:** You must **crank the Length/Period setting down to 5**. This is non-negotiable for clarity. It will help the indicator mark the significant pivots, but remember: the indicator is just a guide. Your eyes must do the real work.
