### 2. The Binary Nature of Market Direction
The market is not a mystery; it moves in only two primary directions. If you cannot identify which way the ship is sailing, you will drown.

| Market Directionality | Definition |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Uptrend** | Price is consistently moving upward, printing higher milestones. |
| **Downtrend** | Price is consistently moving downward, printing lower milestones. |

### 3. Anatomical Components of Market Structure
To navigate like a professional, you must speak the language of price action milestones. Stop guessing and start identifying:

*   **Uptrend Components:**
    *   **HH (Higher High):** A price peak that exceeds the previous peak.
    *   **HL (Higher Low):** A price trough that stays above the previous trough.
*   **Downtrend Components:**
    *   **LH (Lower High):** A price peak that fails to reach the height of the previous peak.
    *   **LL (Lower Low):** A price trough that drops below the previous trough.

**Crucial Rule:** Structure is comparative and requires confirmation. You cannot call a point a Higher High until you see price actually break above the previous high. If price is sitting inside a range, structure does not exist. You must see the breakout to confirm the trend is alive.

### 4. The Standard Rule Set for Trend Continuity
The following "If/Then" relationships are the "Bread and Butter" of my trading system. This is the logic you must burn into your brain if you want to make money.

```text
If Price makes a HL -> Price is expected to make a HH.
If Price makes a HH -> Price is expected to make a HL.
If Price makes a LH -> Price is expected to make a LL.
If Price makes a LL -> Price is expected to make a LH.
```

### 5. Time Frame Hierarchy and Reliability
Market structure is the cheat code because it does not "break" fast on the Higher Time Frame (HTF). If you want stability and trades that actually hold, you respect the hierarchy.

The three essential time frames for validating structure are:
1.  **1 Hour (1H)**
2.  **4 Hour (4H)**
3.  **1 Day (1D)**

**Reliability Note:** The higher the time frame, the more authoritative the rule. Small time frames are noise; they do not respect these structural rules consistently and will lead you into traps. Stick to the 1H, 4H, and Daily to see the real moves.
