## 1. What "Kindergarten-Level" Price Action Really Means

Trades by Sci opens Day 11 by reminding you that ICC is **not** a new complex strategy or some attempt to "trade like the banks." ICC is simply price action explained in the **simplest possible way** — kindergarten level.

**The only building blocks you need:**
- **Swing Highs** — where sellers took control and pushed price down
- **Swing Lows** — where buyers took control and pushed price up

> *"Keep it simple. Market structure is just swing highs and swing lows building up — building bricks. You need clues. Market structure is going to give you those clues. Nothing else."*

**What this means in practice:**
- No indicators. No complex terminology.
- Do NOT overthink. Do NOT ask "what if it moves 8.35%?"
- Stop trying to be creative. **Follow what price does.**
- If something works, do not do the opposite.

## 2. Valid Entry vs. The Break & Retest Trap

This is one of the most critical distinctions in Day 11.

### ❌ Invalid: The Break & Retest (NOT How ICC Works)
Many traders assume ICC is just "break and retest." It is not.

A standard break & retest says:
1. Price breaks a level
2. Price comes back to touch that exact line
3. You enter

**The problem:** If you trade the retest directly, you will often get stopped out because the market needs to sweep **liquidity** first before the real move happens.

### ✅ Valid: The Reaction Entry (ICC Method)

| Step | What Happens | What You Do |
|------|-------------|-------------|
| **Indication** | Price breaks a swing high or swing low | Observe. Do NOT enter yet. |
| **Correction** | Price pulls back, grabs liquidity, shakes out weak hands | Watch on the 15m/5m for clues |
| **Reaction** | Price proves it won't go further; forms higher low (or lower high) | Enter on the second push |

> *"All I'm doing is looking at where the market has a level, seeing what price does under that level, what type of reaction it gives, and trading only that reaction."*

## 3. The Core Clue: Higher Lows & Lower Highs

The market tells you when it's about to reverse through **structure shifts**. Sci avoids jargon like CHoCH or BOS — instead he describes it plainly:

### Spotting a Trend Reversal (Bullish Example)
In a downtrend, price makes:
- Lower High → Lower Low → Lower High → Lower Low → **Lower High → HIGHER LOW** ← This is your warning

When price forms a **higher low** after a series of lower lows, it means:
- Buyers are sitting at that level and **not letting price go lower**
- A battle is happening between buyers and sellers
- The trend is becoming **skeptical** and may be turning

> *"Higher lows are indications to show you that price is looking to go bullish — buyers are sitting at this level. Buyers are holding pretty strong here."*

### The Exact Entry Trigger
**The buy trigger** happens when price breaks **above** the previous lower high (where sellers were sitting).

- Above that level → Bullish momentum confirmed
- Do not second-guess it. Do not wait for indicators.
- Force yourself to accept: *"Anything above this swing high is bullish."*

  - ✅ 4H structure is bullish above this level

## 6. Timeframe Correlation — The Drill-Down Method

| Timeframe | Purpose | What You Look For |
|-----------|---------|-------------------|
| **4H / 1H** | Satellite View — Direction & Bias | Swing highs/lows, major reaction levels, where the trend is |
| **15m / 5m** | Microscope View — Entry Precision | Structural shift (higher low), break above sellers |

**The Rule:** You do NOT need to wait for the 4H candle to close.

Once the 4H shows the directional bias (above a reaction level), drop to the 15-minute and enter as soon as the structure confirms it.

> *"My entries normally come from the 15-minute timeframe. It really doesn't matter what timeframe you get the entry on — as long as you know that above or below the levels you're looking for is the right direction."*

## 7. The Boxing Analogy — Why Backtesting is a Trap

This is Sci's most passionate point in Day 11.

**The Problem with Backtesting:**
- Backtesting uses 2019 or 2021 data in a 2025 market
- Markets change **daily** based on real economic events (government shutdowns, Trump policies, Fed decisions)
- Stale data gives you false confidence without real emotional experience

**The Boxing Analogy:**
> *"I do boxing — MMA. My coach had me punch the bag, do drills. I got really good at punching the bag. But the moment I started sparring with my real coach, I froze up. Everything I learned was gone from my head. Trading is the same way."*

- **Backtesting** = punching a bag (you get good at drills, but freeze in real scenarios)
- **Forward testing in live markets** = real sparring (you make mistakes, feel the emotions, and learn faster)

**The Solution:**
- Enter the live markets **every single day**, even if you don't trade
- Watch price move in real time
- Put yourself in the fire — you learn 10x faster from live experience than from replaying old charts

> *"You learn way more from being in real time and watching everything happen in front of you than from backtesting a random Tuesday in 2019 that you don't even know what that day felt like."*

## 9. Glossary — Day 11 Terms

| Term | Definition |
|------|-----------|
| **Kindergarten Price Action** | The ICC method — using only swing highs and lows with no indicators |
| **Indication** | A break of a swing high or low that starts the ICC cycle |
| **Correction** | The pullback after the indication that grabs liquidity before the real move |
| **Reaction** | The entry signal — price proving it won't continue the prior trend |
| **Higher Low** | A low that is higher than the previous low — signals buyers stepping in |
| **Lower High** | A high that is lower than the previous high — signals sellers defending |
| **No Trade Zone** | The area between a swing high and swing low before an indication |
| **Forward Testing** | Trading or observing the live market daily to build real experience |
| **Break & Retest** | A common (but ICC-invalid) entry method that enters on the first pullback |
