**Day 9: Identify Reversals**

Stop trying to be a genie and predicting the exact bottom of a chart. 🧞‍♂️🛑 

Retail traders blow their accounts catching falling knives because they trade what they *think* will happen, instead of what the structure is actually doing. 

If a downtrend is still making lower highs, IT IS STILL A DOWNTREND. 📉

The moment it breaks a lower high? That’s your Indication. But wait for the Correction (the retest) before you jump in. Let the market show its cards first! 🃏
