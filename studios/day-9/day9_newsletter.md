**Stop trying to be a genie.** 

You do not need to predict the reversal. The market will *tell* you when it's reversing. How? By breaking fundamental structure.

If you are in a downtrend, price should be maintaining lower highs. The absolute second that price breaks a lower high, the trend is changing. 

But remember the ICC rule: **That structural break is an Indication, not an entry.**

If you buy the initial breakout, the market will drop back down, hit your stop loss, grab your liquidity, and *then* reverse without you. 

Wait for the **Correction**. Let the market pull back and retest that broken level. When the New York volume steps in (like the 9:30 AM open we saw on Gold) and pushes away from that retest... that is your **Continuation**. That is your safe, high-probability entry.

React to the structure. Don't predict it.
