# Slide 1: The Retail Trap
* **Catching Falling Knives:** Retail traders try to predict the exact top or bottom of a trend.
* **The Result:** Blown accounts and massive losses.
* **The ICC Solution:** Wait for the market to show its cards by breaking fundamental structure.

---

# Slide 2: Fundamental Structure
* **Downtrend Rules:** Must create Lower Highs (LH) and Lower Lows (LL).
* **The Golden Rule:** If price stops making lower highs, the trend is fundamentally changing.
* **The Indication:** The exact moment price breaks ABOVE the previous lower high.

---

# Slide 3: The Indication vs. Entry
* **The Breakout Trap:** Do not enter immediately when the lower high breaks.
* **The Correction Phase:** The market will pull back to grab liquidity (triggering stop-losses) from the emotional breakout buyers.
* **The Retest:** Wait for price to pull back and test the broken level as new support.

---

# Slide 4: Session Timing & The Continuation
* **The London Catalyst:** Often provides the initial push that breaks structure (The Indication).
* **The New York Catalyst (9:30 AM):** Often provides the massive volume needed for the Retest and the resulting Continuation.
* **The Execution:** Enter during the Continuation phase when volume confirms the new trend direction. Target the next major swing level.
