Market Rule or Concept,Definition,Application Strategy,Common Mistakes to Avoid,Underlying Logic/Example,Source
Higher High (HH) Rule,A point where the price reaches a peak higher than the previous peak in an uptrend.,Do not buy at the higher high; wait for the price to create a Higher Low (HL) to enter a position.,Buying at the absolute top of a move because of large candle size or FOMO.,"After a higher high is made, the market naturally looks to correct and find a higher low to gather more 'orders' or 'cash' to continue upward. [1]",[1]
Lower Low (LL) Rule,A point where price reaches a trough lower than the previous trough in a downtrend.,Avoid selling at the lower low; wait for the market to pull back and create a Lower High (LH) before entering a sell.,Selling at the bottom of a move (the 'blood') when the market is due for a correction.,"Selling at a lower low is risky because the daily time frame may not have corrected yet, leading to a massive move against you. [1]",[1]
Time Frame Alignment (Team Strategy),The requirement that multiple time frames show the same directional sentiment before entering a trade.,"Ensure the 4-hour, 1-hour, and 30-minute time frames match. If the 4H is bullish, the lower time frames must also be bullish.",Trying to trade a trend 'solo' when only one time frame shows the signal while others are conflicting.,Price doesn't move solo; it needs a 'team' of multiple buyers/sellers across time frames to create a strong trend. [1],[1]
Market Corrections (Pullbacks),Temporary price reversals against the main trend used to collect more liquidity.,Remain patient during green days in a downtrend or red days in an uptrend; these are just periods for buyers/sellers to 'refuel'.,Getting scared and thinking a minor correction means the market is 'going to hell' or reversing permanently.,A pullback is like being short $2 at a store and going back to the car to grab the rest of the money to complete the purchase. [1],[1]
Resistance and Sellers,The price level where sellers are located and supply outweighs demand.,Recognize that buying into resistance is trading into a pool of sell orders; expect a reaction or a 'swap' of orders.,Assuming price will break through resistance without enough 'buy orders' or volume.,"If sellers want 10 orders and buyers only have 5, the price will hit resistance and sell off until buyers go back to 'the car' (the pullback) to get more money. [1]",[1]
Value and Volume,The economic principle that market movement is driven by how much value participants see in an asset.,"Trade assets where people find value, as value brings volume, and volume brings money (the 'bread and butter').",Trading assets with no volume or trying to force a buy on something the economy currently devalues.,"If gold is going down, people don't see value in it right now. Everything in the market 'wants' to go up, but it only does so if the economy sees value. [1]",[1]
