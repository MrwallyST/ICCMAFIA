1,Trends & Market Structure,Trends consist of repeating higher highs (HH) / higher lows (HL) in uptrends or lower highs (LH) / lower lows (LL) in downtrends. ICC starts with an Indication (new high/low).,"Uptrend rule: If price makes HL, it will make HH; if it makes HH, it will make HL. Downtrend rule: If price makes LH, it will make LL; if it makes LL, it will make LH.","Daily, 4-hour, and 1-hour time frames are the stable higher time frames that do not break fast.",Buy entries should originate from HLs in an uptrend; sell entries should originate from LHs in a downtrend.,[1]
2,Market Structure Rules,Indication is the HH. Avoid buying at HH or selling at LL because a Correction (C) is expected.,Never buy at a HH because a HL is coming. Never sell at a LL because a LH is coming.,Use the 4-hour and 1-hour combination; 30-minute can be added. Daily is for bigger moves but lags.,"High-probability trades require all time frames (4H, 1H, 30m) to align/match in the same market direction.",[2]
3,Indication (ICC Part 1),Indication (I) is a new high/low. Correction (C) is the pullback. Continuation (C) is the final push to target highs/lows.,Indication identifies price direction objectives without needing psychological numbers or Fibonacci tools.,4-hour time frame is used to find the main Indication; 1-hour is used for more detailed structure information.,The entry is triggered at the Indication level (the point where the previous swing high/low is broken).,[3]
4,Understanding Corrections,Corrections consist of LHs in an uptrend pullback or HLs in a downtrend pullback.,A Correction is considered over when the smaller time frame structure shifts back to align with the higher time frame trend.,4H to identify the trend; 1H to monitor flow; 15-minute to find specific entry details and Correction end signs.,Entry triggers when the 15-minute time frame shifts back bullish (for buys) or bearish (for sells) to align with 4H.,[4]
5,Daily & 4H Combo,The 'Daily and 4-hour combo' looks for ICC on the Daily time frame while using the 4H for the precise entry trigger.,Price must not break the 'original creator' (the extreme low/high that initiated the move). Sell entries require a break of a swing low.,Daily and 4-hour combo. Use 15-minute to observe alignment of LHs/HLs within the higher time frame move.,Stop-loss for sells is placed above the previous LH (the creator). Targets are set at the next level of buyers (swing lows).,[5]
6,Breakdown Thoughts & Probability,"Trends continue based on pressure; if sellers break a strong Daily buyer level, probability favors extreme Continuation.",Structure remains valid as long as the HH/HL or LH/LL sequence holds. A break of a LH makes a downtrend invalid.,"Daily, 4-hour, and 1-hour are the 'bread and butter' time frames; all three must correlate for an entry.",Invalidation/Exit: Above the LH for sells. Targets: Use the next major Daily/4H high or low as the Take Profit level.,[6]
