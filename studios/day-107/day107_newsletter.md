## 2. The Foundations of Market Structure

### 2.1. Mission 1: The Rule of Comparables
The moment you open a chart, your first mission is simple: **Find your High (H) and your Low (L).** Without these two points, nothing else exists. You cannot identify a trend or a trade setup without a previous point of comparison. 
*   **Range:** If price is stuck between your established H and L, it is a range. You do not trade here. You wait for price to break out and show its hand.

### 2.2. The Binary Nature of Price Movement
Price only moves in two directions that matter for your P&L:
1.  **Uptrend:** Price is consistently respecting the buyers.
2.  **Downtrend:** Price is "bleeding" as sellers take control.

### 2.3. Anatomy of a Trend
Master these four structure points. They are your "cheat code" because structure on the Higher Timeframes (HTF) does not break fast.

| Structure Point | Abbreviation | Definition | Trend Type |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Higher High** | HH | A peak higher than the previous peak. | Uptrend |
| **Higher Low** | HL | A trough higher than the previous trough. | Uptrend |
| **Lower High** | LH | A peak lower than the previous peak. | Downtrend |
| **Lower Low** | LL | A trough lower than the previous trough. | Downtrend |

## 3. The Core Strategy: The ICC Framework

The ICC framework (Indication, Correction, Continuation) is a "rinse and repeat" cycle. It is the language the market uses to tell you its plan.

### 3.1. I - Indication (The Answer)
The Indication is a breakout. It is the market’s "answer" to where it wants to go. 
*   **The Entry Zone:** While you do not chase the initial impulsive move (the "breakout"), the **Indication Level** (the previous swing high or low that was broken) becomes your entry trigger once the correction ends.
*   **Case Study (BTC):** In the BTC example at 71k, the "Blue Line" was the indication level. Once price broke above that swing high, it signaled bullish intent. The level of that break is where the entry was built.

### 3.2. C - Correction (The Store/Car Analogy)
The Correction is the pullback (the HL or LH). 
*   **The Analogy:** Imagine you are at a store. You want to buy a product for $20, but you only have $4. You have to go back to the car to grab the remaining $16. 
*   The market does the same. It hits resistance, realizes it doesn't have enough "money" (orders), and pulls back to gather more liquidity before pushing through.

### 3.3. C - Continuation (The Finish)
This is where price finishes what it started, targeting the previous high/low or creating a new one. The trend repeats this cycle: **Indication -> Correction -> Continuation -> New Indication.**

## 4. Multi-Timeframe Correlation (The "Team" Concept)

Price does not move solo; it needs a "team" of timeframes to align sentiment. If your timeframes don't match, you don't have a trade.

### 4.1. The Hierarchy of Flow
*   **4-Hour (4H) - Identification:** Used to identify the overall trend and the current ICC phase.
*   **1-Hour (1H) - Flow:** Used to monitor the "flow" of the trend and ensure the 4H sentiment is being respected.
*   **15-Minute (15m) - Details:** This is where you find the specific turning points.

### 4.2. The Daily/4H Combo
This is for the swing traders. Use the **Daily chart** to see the overall ICC phase. If the Daily makes a new LL, you are simply waiting for a Daily correction to finish so you can catch the massive move back down on the 4H.

## 5. Operational Rules and Entry Criteria

### 5.1. The "Golden Rules" of Execution
*   **Never buy at a Higher High (HH).** Chasing the peak is how beginners get trapped.
*   **Never sell at a Lower Low (LL).** Selling the bottom of the "bleed" is suicide.
*   **Buy at the Higher Low (HL).**
*   **Sell at the Lower High (LH).**

### 5.2. The Creator and the Product
The "Original Creator" is the specific low/high that caused the new move (the Product). 
*   **The Rule:** If the "Product" is healthy, price should **never backtrack** into the Creator’s territory. 
*   Sellers/Buyers don't want to go back into the red. The moment price backtracks past the Creator, the plan is gone and the trend is likely reversing.

### 5.3. Mastering the 15m "Details"
To find the exact moment a 4H/1H correction ends, look at the 15m. During a 4H correction (pullback), the 15m will look like a "mini-trend" in the opposite direction. 
*   **The Trigger:** You are waiting for the 15m to "break back" and form its own smaller-scale ICC pattern that aligns with the HTF. When the 15m structure shifts from bearish back to bullish (in an uptrend), the correction is over.

## 6. Supply, Demand, and the Business of Trading

### 6.1. Participants
*   **Resistance = Sellers.**
*   **Support = Buyers.**

### 6.2. The Business Analogy
A downtrend is essentially a "bleeding business." 
*   Think of price as a company's value. If investors (buyers) don't see value at a support level, they won't provide the cash to keep it afloat. If the company (the asset) can't manage its "support" properly, it falls through.
*   **Value brings Volume; Volume brings Money.** You trade assets where people find value because that’s where the high-probability volume lives.
*   **Case Study (Gold):** When Gold breaks a Daily level, it shows that sellers have bypassed the buyers' "cash" reserves. Sellers are now stronger than buyers, and the business is bleeding toward the next major low.

## 7. Risk Management and Trade Invalidation

### 7.1. Stop-Loss Placement
Your stop-loss is your protection against "backtracking."
*   **For Sells:** Above the previous **Lower High (The Creator)**.
*   **For Buys:** Below the previous **Higher Low (The Creator)**.

### 7.2. Identifying Invalidation
A setup is invalid the moment price violates the Creator. If you are in a sell and price breaks back above the LH that started the move, the sellers' plan has failed. Exit immediately. Don't let your ego tell you it might come back.

### 7.3. Session Pressure
You need volume to push price through resistance. 
*   **The New York Session:** Specifically, the **9:30 AM open** is highly reliable for the volume needed to finish an ICC move. Trade during these high-pressure sessions (London/NY) to ensure the "team" is active.
