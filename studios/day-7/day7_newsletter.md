### 2. The Three Pillars of Price Action
To successfully navigate the market, you must categorize the environment into one of three primary states. Each state dictates the rules of engagement.

1.  **Uptrends:** Defined by a consistent structural progression of **Higher Highs (HH)** and **Higher Lows (HL)**.
2.  **Downtrends:** Defined by a consistent structural progression of **Lower Lows (LL)** and **Lower Highs (LH)**.
3.  **Consolidation:** The state where the market "breaks the rules." Highs and lows are violated sideways without clear expansion.

> **Warning: The "Criminal Friends" of the Market**
> You must avoid consolidation zones. In life, you stay away from "criminal friends" because they break the rules and invite trouble; the same logic applies to a sideways market. Consolidation is the "Shakeup of the Coke Bottle"—it is a period where the market is accumulating the massive capital/liquidity required to break through major "brick walls." Do not get trapped in the shakeup.

### 3. The ICC Methodology: Indication, Correction, Continuation
The ICC strategy is a high-probability three-step framework designed to align your entries with institutional volume and liquidity shifts.

#### Section 3.1: The Indication (The Explosion)
The Indication is the "Explosion." It occurs when price breaks through a "Brick Wall" (a major swing high or low) on the 1H or 4H timeframe. These swing levels hold significant weight because they represent price points that require massive capital to break. 

**Characteristics of a Valid Indication:**
*   **Session Volume:** A valid indication must occur during high-volume sessions (e.g., 8:00 AM for London or 9:30 AM for New York). Movements outside these times are often "fake volume."
*   **Momentum:** It must be a clear burst of energy, representing the cap flying off the shaken Coke bottle.
*   **Information Only:** An indication is **information, not an entry signal**. It reveals the market’s hand but is not the time to execute.

#### Section 3.2: The Correction (The Liquidity Trap)
Following the "Explosion," the market enters the "Fizz" or "Mess" phase. This is the Correction. The market needs to "dip into the pool" of money (liquidity) created by the stop-losses of early breakout traders to fuel a sustainable trend.

**Psychology of the Correction**

| Feature | Breakout / FOMO Traders | Professional Patience |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Action** | Jump in at the peak of the breakout. | Wait for the "mess" to clear and liquidity to be grabbed. |
| **Motivation** | Fear of missing the explosive move. | Seeking structural confirmation and cash fuel. |
| **Outcome** | Liquidated or stopped out during the "fizz." | Enters after early traders provide the necessary liquidity. |

#### Section 3.3: The Continuation (The Real Move)
The Continuation is the objective. Once the correction has "cleaned up the mess" and tapped into the liquidity pool, price flips its internal structure back toward the original direction of the indication. This is the highest-probability entry point.

Precision requires a "Satellite vs. Microscope" approach to ensure all data points correlate.

*   **Higher Timeframes (1H / 4H) - The Satellite:** Used to identify the big picture, locate "Brick Walls," and confirm the "Explosion" (Indication).
*   **Lower Timeframes (15M / 5M) - The Microscope:** Used to monitor the internal "body" of the correction. 

**The Surgeon’s X-Ray:** 
If a patient has a headache, the Doctor (Satellite) identifies the symptom. The Surgeon (Microscope) uses an **X-ray** to see the internal cause. You must look "inside" the 1H candle by scaling down to the 5M chart to see the exact moment the internal market structure shifts from correction back to continuation.

### 6. Advanced Market Mechanics: The Contract Swap
Markets are composed of "Brick Walls." 
*   **Swing Lows:** Guarded by **Buyers**. Price above a swing low maintains bullish potential.
*   **Swing Highs:** Guarded by **Sellers**. Price below a swing high maintains bearish potential.

**The iPhone 16 Analogy:**
Trading is a contractual swap. If you are selling an iPhone 16, you don't look for someone who already has one; you look for a buyer. In the market, your profit targets are located at previous swing highs/lows because that is where the opposite party's money is sitting. To exit a buy, you must take it to the **Sellers** (at swing highs). To exit a sell, you must take it to the **Buyers** (at swing lows).
