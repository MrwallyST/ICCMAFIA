**2. THE CORE CONCEPT: DEFINING THE CORRECTION**
**(Visual: Text overlay appears in bold: ICC – INDICATION, CORRECTION, CONTINUATION.)**

**3. THE VISUAL ANALOGY: THE COKE BOTTLE**
**(Visual: Split-screen. Left side: A hand shaking a Coke bottle. Right side: A 1-Hour chart showing sideways consolidation.)**

**Audio:** Think of market mechanics like a Coke bottle. It’s a three-step process:

*   **The Shakeup:** This is consolidation. Price is sideways, building extreme pressure.
*   **The Explosion:** This is the **Indication**. The cap flies off, and price breaks the level. **(Visual: The Coke bottle explodes while the chart shows a massive breakout candle.)**
*   **The Mess:** This is the **Correction**. The fizz goes everywhere. It’s volatile, it’s messy, and it’s where most of you act like dumbasses and lose your accounts.

**Audio:** Write this down: "The indication and the correction are two sides of the same coin." You cannot have the explosion without the messy cleanup. Stop chasing the fizz.
