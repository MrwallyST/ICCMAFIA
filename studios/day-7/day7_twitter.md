**The ICC Methodology Overview**

The ICC strategy is a three-step framework used to keep you on the right side of the trend. It’s not just a setup; it’s a continuous cycle:

1. **Indication:** The signal fire.
2. **Correction:** The liquidity grab.
3. **Continuation:** The logical entry.

Master this cycle, and you stop chasing "noise."

***

**Step 1: The Indication (The Signal Fire)**

The **Indication** occurs when price breaks above or below significant swing highs or lows on the 1-hour or 4-hour charts.

*   **Swing High:** The highest point reached before price pivots.
*   **Swing Low:** The lowest point reached before price pivots.

**NO ENTRY ON THE EXPLOSION.** 
Indication is *information*, not an entry signal. It tells you where the momentum is starting, but it is not time to buy yet.

***

**The Coke Bottle Analogy**

Think of market dynamics like a Coke bottle. Levels cost money to break, and the market must build pressure first:

*   **The Shakeup:** Consolidation where pressure/money builds.
*   **The Explosion:** The Indication (the cap flies off).
*   **The Mess:** The fizz goes everywhere (the Correction).

The Indication and Correction are two sides of the same coin. You cannot have the explosion without the inevitable messy cleanup.

***

**Step 2: The Correction (The Fuel Grab)**

A **Correction** is a calculated pause after a breakout. It isn’t a trend failing; it’s the market seeking "fuel." 

The market cannot break major levels without capital. It intentionally creates a "cleanup" move to grab **Liquidity**—the stop-losses of emotional traders—to fuel the real, sustainable trend.

***

**The Psychology of the Trap**

The market doesn't want you to "steal" from it; it wants your capital. Who gets caught in the Correction?

*   **Early Breakout Traders:** Those jumping in the millisecond price moves.
*   **Late FOMO Traders:** Those piling in at the top, terrified of being left behind.

The market views these emotional positions as a "pool of money." It must dip into that pool to collect the cash required to move the trend further.

***

**Timeframe Correlation (Satellite vs. Microscope)**

To trade ICC, you must align your views:

*   **1H/4H (Satellite View):** Spot the big picture, consolidation, and the initial Indication. 
*   **15m/5m (Microscope View):** Monitor the "mess" of the correction. 

Zooming in allows you to see exactly when the cleanup is finishing and the trend is ready to resume.

***

**Step 3: The Continuation (The Real Entry)**

**ENTRY RULE:**
The **Continuation** is your entry. You only enter when the lower timeframe (15m/5m) flips back to align with the higher timeframe (HTF) trend.

**Wait for the 15m to flip:** 
Break the Lower High -> Confirm the Bullish Shift.
Align the microscope with the satellite.

***

**The Golden Rule of Structural Integrity**

Price will not break its HTF structure unless it is intended to reverse. 

*   **The Rule:** Price must NOT break its 1H/4H support during a 15m correction. If it does, the trade is void.
*   **Stop-Loss:** Place your SL below the **Higher Timeframe** structural level, not just the local 15m low.

A successful Continuation creates a *new* Indication, starting the ICC cycle all over again.
