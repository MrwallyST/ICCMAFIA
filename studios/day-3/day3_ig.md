Before you place a single trade, you need to answer these 3 questions:

✅ WHERE is price in the structure right now? (Above or below the last swing?)
✅ WHAT liquidity is resting above and below current price? (Equal highs, swing highs, trendline touches?)
✅ WHERE is the nearest high probability reaction zone? (Order blocks, fair value gaps, old structure)

Once you can answer these 3 questions on a blank chart in under 60 seconds, you're trading like an institution.
