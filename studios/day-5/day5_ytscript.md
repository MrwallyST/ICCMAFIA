## 2. THE CORE FUNDAMENTALS: SELLERS VS. BUYERS

**[Screen Share: Clean chart with a High and a Low marked]**

To understand entries, you have to understand the market at a kindergarten level. It’s just buyers versus sellers. 

*   **Highs:** These are the levels where **Sellers** reside. It’s the highest point price can reach before sellers step in to drive it back down.
*   **Lows:** These are the levels where **Buyers** reside. It’s the lowest point price reaches before buyers decide the price is right to drive it back up.

### The "Brick Wall" and the "Boss Level"
Think of these levels as a **Brick Wall**. Price hits a level and stops because it can’t move through the volume of orders sitting there. When buyers finally break through a high, they haven’t just moved the price; they’ve cleared a level of sellers and reached another **Boss Level**. 

**[On-Screen Text: THE LAW OF STRUCTURE]**

> **ANYTHING ABOVE A SWING LOW = POTENTIAL BULLISHNESS**
> **ANYTHING BELOW A SWING HIGH = POTENTIAL BEARISHNESS**

## 4. UNDERSTANDING LIQUIDITY (THE CHANEL BAG ANALOGY)

**[On-Screen Graphic: A Chanel Bag next to a Liquidity Pool icon]**

Liquidity is just a fancy word for **GRABBING MORE MONEY.** 

The market is predatory. When you make money, you are essentially **stealing** from the market. The market doesn't want you to steal; it wants you to lose so it can collect *your* money.

> ### THE CHANEL BAG ANALOGY
> You see a Chanel bag you want, but you don’t have the cash. What do you do? You go out, you make some more money, and then you come back to the store to buy it. Then, a "Boss Level" bag comes out—even more expensive. You have to go grab *more* money before you can reach that higher level.

The market does the same thing. It "liquidates" traders—hitting their stop losses—to collect enough money to fuel the move to the next level of sellers or buyers. It’s not just moving; it’s hunting.

## 5. TRADING PSYCHOLOGY: THE TRAP OF FOMO AND REVENGE

Traders fail because they lack emotional control. It usually goes like this:

1.  **FOMO & Greed:** This happens during the **Breakout (The First Move)**. You see a green candle, you panic because you think you’re missing the moon, and you jump in with no plan.
2.  **Revenge Trading:** This happens during the **Liquidation (The Correction)**. The market pulls back to grab money, hits your stop, and you get angry. You try to "get back" at the market by flipping directions at the worst possible time.

**Think Long-Term:**
We are trading **economies and governments**, not 15-minute Instagram clips. 
*   **Stick with one thing:** If the trend is bullish, look for buys. Stop looking for buys on Monday and sells on Tuesday. 
*   **Avoid the Noise:** Holding a play based on the 1H/4H is how you stop the consistent losses. Stop taking 30 trades a week.

## 6. THE REFINED ENTRY CRITERIA

**[Screen Share: 15m chart highlighting an entry point]**

Once you have the setup on the 1H or 4H, scale down to the **15-minute** to find the momentum.

### The Entry Laws:
*   **The Momentum Kick-In:** Your entry only happens where the bullish momentum *initially* kicked in. 
*   **THE SECOND PUSH RULE:** We **DO NOT** trade under the previous high. Period. If price is back under a high, it has selling potential. That is a No-Trade Zone. You wait for the "Second Push" back above the level to prove the buyers have actually cleared the "Boss Level."
*   **Stop Loss Placement:** Put it under the "Higher Low." If the trend is truly bullish, the market has no business breaking that level.

## 7. CHART ANALYSIS: INDICATION, CORRECTION, CONTINUATION

**[Screen Share: NASDAQ 1H Chart]**

Let's look at the current NASDAQ chart. If you don't understand this after 60 days, you’re a fucking dumbass. It’s that simple.

1.  **INDICATION:** We identify the 1-hour high. Price breaks that structure. That is our "Indication" that the market *wants* to move.
2.  **CORRECTION:** We wait for the liquidation. This is the pullback where the market collects money from the FOMO traders.
3.  **CONTINUATION:** We look for the move back over the level. 

**Host:** Look at this example. We didn't take the trade on the first move. Why? Because there was no **Second Push** above the level yet. Sellers were still sitting at that high. We only enter when the buyers prove they are stronger than the "Brick Wall" of sellers.
