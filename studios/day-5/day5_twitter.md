**Tweet 1**
Stop asking about pips, TradingView Premium, or magic indicators. If you can’t grasp market reactions, you’re just exit liquidity. I haven't had a boss in four years because I stopped overcomplicating the charts and started thinking for myself. Day 5 is about entries. Listen up, or stay broke. 

**Tweet 2**
Let’s start at a kindergarten level. Trading is just identifying who is in control.
*   **Highs (Sellers):** The highest point price reaches before the "Boss" (the seller) steps in to drive it down.
*   **Lows (Buyers):** The level where buyers see value and step in to drive price back up.
These aren't "lines." These are zones where the heavy hitters live. 

**Tweet 3**
The "Brick Wall" Principle is your law. I’ve traded NASDAQ exclusively for four years using this mindset. You find your swing levels on the **1-hour, 4-hour, and Daily** charts. These are the **Keys**.

> "Anything above a swing low = potential bullishness. Anything below a swing high = potential bearishness."

**Tweet 4**
Understand Liquidity: The "Chanel Bag" Analogy. 👜
The market sees a target (the bag) but doesn't have the money to buy it yet. To get the capital, it has to "go to the bank"—which is your stop loss. It liquidates retail traders to grab the cash it needs to make the real move. It’s not a conspiracy; it’s just how the business works.

**Tweet 5**
Retail traders fail because they fall for the same two traps:
1.  **FOMO & Greed:** You jump into the initial breakout because you're scared of missing out. You're buying at the ceiling like a fool.
2.  **Revenge Trading:** You lose on the buy, get emotional, and immediately flip to a sell right as the market is actually preparing to move in your original direction.

**Tweet 6**
The Reaction Level Entry Strategy. Stop guessing and wait for the "Second Push." 
- [ ] Identify the swing high where sellers held control.
- [ ] Wait for price to break that level (Sellers are swept).
- [ ] **Mandatory:** Wait for the **second push** back above that level to confirm the reaction.
- [ ] Scale to the **15-minute timeframe** for the final execution.
- [ ] SL goes under the most recent higher low.

**Tweet 7**
If you are "Under the High," you are in **Seller Territory**. The Boss is still in the room. Even if it looks like it’s moving up, if it hasn't cleared that previous swing high and held it, the sellers can dump it at any second. You only play when the Boss has been evicted and **Momentum** is on your side.

Use the 1H and 4H charts to think long-term. One solid play on a Monday can pay for your whole week if you actually have the patience to hold.
