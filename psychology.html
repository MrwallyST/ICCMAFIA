🧪 Trading Bias Library
🔥
FOMO — Fear of Missing Out
High Risk
You see price ripping and feel the urge to jump in mid-move. Your brain tells you "this one is different." It isn't. Smart money already positioned at the bottom. You're buying their exit.
ICC Fix: There is no trade without a Correction. If price hasn't pulled back, there is no setup. Wait for the next ICC cycle — it always comes.
😤
Revenge Trading
Account Killer
You took a loss and immediately want it back. You double your size or take the next marginal setup just to recover. This is pure emotion — your prefrontal cortex has gone offline.
ICC Fix: 2 losses = close the platform. No exceptions. Come back tomorrow with a reset mind. The market doesn't owe you your money back today.
😑
Boredom Trading
Sneaky
You've been staring at the chart for 2 hours waiting for a setup. Nothing is forming. You start seeing patterns that aren't there — you need the dopamine hit of a trade.
ICC Fix: Ask: "Does this meet ALL 3 phases — Indication, Correction, Continuation?" If you're asking, it doesn't. Close the chart and take a walk.
😰
Anxiety & Hesitation
Missed Trades
A perfect A+ setup forms. You freeze. You second-guess. By the time you convince yourself, the move is over. Fear of being wrong costs you more than most losses.
ICC Fix: Pre-define your entry criteria. When all 3 conditions are met, execution is mechanical — not emotional. Backtest until you trust the setup.
📈
Recency Bias
Distortion
After 3 wins, you feel invincible and oversize. After 3 losses, you feel like the strategy is broken and cut your size or stop trading. Both are reactions to recent noise, not data.
ICC Fix: Judge performance over 50+ trades minimum. A strategy with a 55% win rate and 1:2 R:R can have 5-loss streaks — that's math, not failure.
🏆
Overconfidence
Post-Win Risk
You had 3 green days in a row and start to feel like the market is yours. You increase size, loosen your rules, trade outside your session. The market will humble you quickly.
ICC Fix: Your rules don't change after wins. Same risk per trade. Same session. Same process. Consistency built the wins — don't break it when it's working.
🎰
Loss Aversion
Distorts R:R
You move your stop loss wider to "give it more room." You let losers run hoping they'll come back. Meanwhile you cut winners early to "lock in" the dopamine. This inverts your R:R over time.
ICC Fix: Set your stop at the Correction extreme BEFORE entering. Never move it wider. Once TP1 hits, move stop to breakeven. The rules protect you from yourself.
🔁
Confirmation Bias
Selective Vision
You decided the market is going up and now you only see bullish signals. You ignore the clear bearish structure on the HTF because it contradicts your bias.
ICC Fix: Always read the HTF first — with no directional opinion. Let the chart tell you the Indication. Then zoom in. Direction follows structure, not your feelings.
