Printable Desk Reference

The ICC Master Blueprint

Tape this to your monitor. Mechanical rules, strict psychology, and zero FOMO.

🧠

1. The Mechanical Framework

Phase 1 INDICATION
Wait for a full candle body to close beyond a major 1H or 4H swing pivot. Wicks do NOT count. This tells you intent. Do not trade this move.
Phase 2 CORRECTION
Price pulls back to re-test the origin of the Indication push (OB, FVG, or previous liquidity). You are waiting for the trap to be set for early buyers/sellers.
Phase 3 CONTINUATION
Wait for a small timeframe (1m/5m) change of character (CHoCH) inside the Correction zone. Your entry is the subsequent Break of Structure (BOS).
🪖

2. Defeating FOMO & Psychology

Brain Hack The "Fear of Missing Out" Illusion
When you see a giant green candle, your brain screams "BUY!". Reality: The smart money already bought at the bottom. Buying a vertical candle is donating your money to them. Wait for the Correction.
Mantra "Every day brings a new setup."
If you miss the entry by 2 seconds, let it go. Chasing a trade destroys your Risk:Reward ratio. The market will open again tomorrow. Your capital must survive until then.
Self-Check The Boredom Trap
Are you taking this trade because it perfectly meets the I-C-C criteria above, or because you've been staring at the screen for two hours and want dopamine? If the latter, walk away.
🎯

3. Execution & Targets

Stop Loss Placement
Your Stop Loss goes exactly 1 tick below/above the extreme of the Correction phase. Never move your stop loss wider once set.
Take Profit 1 (Partial)
Target the local high/low of the Indication phase. Take 50-80% of your position off the table here to secure profits.
Take Profit 2 (Runner)
Leave a runner targeting the "Final Boss" (higher timeframe liquidity pool). Move Stop Loss to breakeven once TP1 is hit.
🛡️

4. Non-Negotiable Risk Rules

The 1% Rule
Never risk more than 1% of your total account on a single setup. If you have a $10,000 account, your stop loss should equal $100.
Daily Drawdown Limit
If you lose two trades in a single session, shut the computer down. Your psychology is compromised. You will revenge trade.
Session Timing
Only trade your designated high-volume session (e.g., NY open 9:30 AM - 11:30 AM EST). Trading the "dead zones" leads to chop-outs.
